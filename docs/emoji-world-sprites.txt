NOSTRUX HOMESTEAD: EMOJI THAT APPEAR IN THE 3D WORLD
====================================================

Only the ones that float above an object in the scene. HUD cell icons and
inline text emoji are deliberately left out.

How they are drawn today: the emoji is painted to a 64px canvas and used as
the texture on a billboard sprite, so it always faces the camera. Replacing
one means dropping a png in and pointing the texture at it.

FORMAT: 256x256 png, transparent. Drawn flat on, no perspective, since it
turns to face the camera from every angle. It renders at roughly 40 to 60
pixels on screen, so it has to read at thumbnail size. Bold silhouette,
few colours, no thin lines, no text.

The coin is already art rather than an emoji (/ui/coin.png). Same idea.

Not tied to a good. These say something needs you.
======================================================================

  EMOJI  MEANING                     WHERE                     SUGGESTED FILE
  💧     this plot is dry            over a planted plot       state/needs-water.png
  ❗     a fish is biting            over the bobber           state/bite.png
  🚧     still being built           over a construction site  state/building.png
  ❔     no icon set                 over an unfinished item   state/unknown.png

  The bite marker is the most time critical thing in the game. It appears for
  a short window and the player has to notice it and click. Make it loud.

WHERE TO START
======================================================================

Section 1 first. Eleven images, they are the largest sprites on screen, and
they are what the farm looks like at a glance when everything is producing.

Then the four in section 2, especially the bite marker.

Section 3 is the long tail. Junk and treasure matter more than the fish,
because fish already leap as real 3D models and the sprite rarely shows.

TOTAL: 13 ready markers, 4 state markers, 44 catch markers.
