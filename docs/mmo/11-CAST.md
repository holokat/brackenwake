# Brackenwake: the cast

The companion to `10-STORY.md`. That book says what each zone is about. This
one says who is standing in it, and what they will talk to you about.

Every person below is written to one frame, because the talk window will carry
all of them the same way:

- **Who.** Name, role, age, one line of how they look and sound.
- **Wants.** The one thing that drives them. Everything they say bends toward it.
- **Knows.** What they can tell you that nobody else can.
- **Offers.** Trade, training, a service, a passage. What the shop side of the
  talk window shows. Where it says *nothing*, the person is not a vendor and
  the window is only talk.
- **Talks about.** The topics, in the order you can earn them. Each topic is
  gated by a fact from `story.js` (in brackets) and sets one when it ends. A
  topic with no bracket is open to a stranger.
- **Tell.** The habit that makes them a person and not a shop: the thing they
  do while you stand there.

Voice is given as one line they would say. Every character gets a different
music. The Vale talks plain and short. The kingdom's remnants talk in old
phrases. The rim talks in whatever three hundred years alone has done to them.

The six who carry the story (Maud, Tam, Hesk, Ysolt, Fern, the Ashen King) are
in `10-STORY.md` section 4 and are only sketched here where they stand.

## Act I: The Hedge

### The Bracken Vale

**Maud Fallow.** Hedge-reeve, seventy. Grey shawl, a walking stick she does not
lean on, a way of looking at you as if reading a stone. Voice: *"The stones
said a name. I do not have to like it."*
- **Wants.** The Vale kept. Then, slowly, to stop lying to you about how.
- **Knows.** What the Sink is. What her sister was. Why the stones named you.
- **Offers.** Nothing. She is not a shop.
- **Talks about.** The stones and your name. The gap in the hedge and the six
  words. Each return: what the Vale has lost since you left [each act]. Her
  sister [read:tally]. What a keeper is [act:4]. Whether you should let Tam sit
  [at the chair].
- **Tell.** She touches the nearest boundary stone before she answers anything.

**Osric Hale.** Innkeeper of the Bracken Arms, fifties, a big man gone soft, an
apron that was white once. Voice: *"Every Walker sits in that chair before they
go. Every one of them orders the same thing. Sit down."*
- **Wants.** To have a Walker come back, once, before he dies.
- **Knows.** What every Walker for forty years said the night before leaving.
  Tam's father's last words in the Vale: "back by harvest."
- **Offers.** Food, drink, a bed that heals, the trade channel's board.
- **Talks about.** The last Walker and the cloak [met:maud]. What the Vale is
  saying about you [each act, varies]. Tam's father, if Tam is with you
  [met:tam]. A toast, on your return from each act, with the whole room.
- **Tell.** He wipes the same glass the whole conversation.

**Bett Colley.** Blacksmith, forties, forearms like hawsers, burn-freckled,
laughs once a day at most. Voice: *"That edge is a disgrace. Give it here."*
- **Wants.** To have made something that mattered. She taught you and worries
  it was not enough.
- **Knows.** Iron. That the Iron Shoulder's Hild is the only smith who works the
  cold metals, and that Hild was her master's master.
- **Offers.** Repairs, copper and iron work, blacksmithing training to 50.
- **Talks about.** Your kit, honestly. Hild and the Shoulder [act:2]. The
  coldiron sword, which she will not touch [has:coldsword]. Starfall, which she
  will not have in the forge [has:starfall].
- **Tell.** She takes whatever weapon you are holding and tests the edge on her
  thumbnail while you talk.

**Wenna Pryce.** Healer, thirties, quick hands, quicker eyes, a satchel that
smells of comfrey. Voice: *"You are bleeding on my floor. Talk after."*
- **Wants.** Nobody in the Vale to die of something she could have stopped.
- **Knows.** What the Wake does to a body: she has treated three people who came
  back through the hedge changed and buried two.
- **Offers.** Healing, resurrection, bandages, healing training to 50.
- **Talks about.** What you can and cannot survive out there. The changed ones
  [act:1, after the first night]. What the Wake does to a keeper, which she
  has guessed [act:4].
- **Tell.** She checks your pulse at the wrist while she is speaking, whether
  you asked or not.

**Old Grist** is a boar and the **Sexton** is a skeleton, and neither talks,
but the Sexton rings the church bell when he sees you and Osric says the bell
has rung every night since the Walker left.

### The Mill Run

**Tam Weir.** Fourteen. Slingshot, a map of the Adit drawn on a flour sack,
never quiet. Voice: *"I have been down all three. Nobody else has. Well. One
other."*
- **Wants.** To go with you. To find out what happened to his father. Later, to
  be worth his name.
- **Knows.** The Adit's cuts and the wall in the third that is not the miners'.
- **Offers.** Nothing to sell. In a fight he is a slingshot and a warning.
- **Talks about.** The Adit. His father [after Thornwood]. His name [after
  Wynn]. Whether he is a Weir, and what that means [read:tally]. Hesk
  [hesk:gone]. The chair [act:4].
- **Tell.** He is always slightly ahead of you on the road.

**Ivy Weir.** Tam's mother, the miller, forties, flour to the elbow, tired in
the way that has stopped being tired and become a shape. Voice: *"He will
follow you. I know it. I am asking you to make sure he can come home."*
- **Wants.** Her son alive. She has already lost her husband to the same road.
- **Knows.** Exactly where Tam's father went and when: north, to Ashen Moor, to
  find the monk who could read the old marks.
- **Offers.** Flour, bread, the mill's storeroom as a stash.
- **Talks about.** Tam. Her husband and the monk [met:tam]. The wall in the
  third cut, which her husband found first [saw:weirmark]. Tam's name, which
  she never told him meant anything [read:tally].
- **Tell.** She keeps working the whole time. The mill does not stop for talk.

**Gower Blane.** Foreman of the Millrun Adit, sixties, one eye, a ledger, a
belief that most problems are rock. Voice: *"Copper pays. Tin pays. Whatever is
behind that wall does not pay, so I have not moved it."*
- **Wants.** The Adit to keep paying. He is not a bad man; he is a foreman.
- **Knows.** The third cut's wall, the goblins who have moved in behind it, and
  the price of every ore on the continent.
- **Offers.** Pickaxes, ore purchase, mining training to 40.
- **Talks about.** Ore prices. The goblin chief who wears a stone as a
  breastplate [entered:adit]. The wall, and that "the marks on it are the same
  as on the ford-stone in the Vale, and I never liked that" [saw:weirmark].
- **Tell.** He does sums in the ledger while you talk and tells you the answer
  at the end, whether it was about you or not.

**Sallow Pike** is the goblin warrior in the third cut. He does not talk, but
he has scratched the weir-mark into his own face, and the miners have a rhyme
about him that Gower will not repeat.

### Saltmere

**Ysolt of the Strand**, passing through. Voice here: *"The water is not
rising. It is looking for something."* She sells salvage and says one true
thing and leaves. Her whole self is in the Broken Strand.

**Nance Cockle.** Cockler, sixty, bare feet in all weathers, a voice like a
gull. Matriarch of the Saltmere hamlet. Voice: *"The Steps go down to the
King's floor, and the King came up to count them, and that's the rhyme, and
that's all it is."*
- **Wants.** The tide to keep its times. It has not, lately.
- **Knows.** The cocklers' rhyme, which is the first mention of a king. The
  tide tables, which are the story's clock for the Steps.
- **Offers.** Shellfish, salt, fishing training to 40.
- **Talks about.** The tides and when the Steps show. The rhyme, if you ask
  twice. The drowned man on the Steps at high water, whom she calls the Reeve
  and will not look at [saw:steps]. Ysolt, whom she trusts and does not like.
- **Tell.** She never stops sorting shells into two baskets by a rule you cannot
  work out.

**Piran Cockle.** Nance's grandson, twenty, restless, wants to leave and has
nowhere to go. Voice: *"There is a door at the bottom of the Steps. I have
touched it. Do not tell her."*
- **Wants.** Out. He will ask to come with you and should not be let.
- **Knows.** The door at the bottom of the Weir Steps, because he has dived to
  it at the lowest tide of the year.
- **Offers.** He will dive for anything you drop in the sound, for a price.
- **Talks about.** The door [saw:steps]. What is carved on it, which he drew
  from memory and got half right [has:key1 corrects him]. The Drowned Coast,
  which he wants to see [act:3, he begs].
- **Tell.** He is always wet.

**The Reeve.** The drowned man who stands on the Steps at high water. Named
encounter, a Drowned. He does not talk, but if you stand on the top step at
high water and do not go down, he turns his head toward Saltmere's hamlet and
raises one hand, and Nance, if asked, says that was her great-grandfather's
gesture for "all in."

### The Sedge Flats

**Granny Eel.** The fen-family's grandmother at the Drowned Mill, eighties,
lives on the stilt-house's top floor and has not come down in a decade. Voice:
*"The river went south in my mother's mother's time. I was told. Two hundred
and eighty years, near enough. Now you know something."*
- **Wants.** To be listened to once before she dies. Everyone in the family has
  heard everything.
- **Knows.** The date. The millstone with the charm cut into it. That the fen
  has been getting deeper every year of her life.
- **Offers.** Nothing. She is talk.
- **Talks about.** The river moving. The date, if asked properly. The millstone
  [entered:mill]. Long Sarah, whom she remembers as a normal size [killed:sarah,
  she is furious]. The Hollow Hills, where the river went [act:2].
- **Tell.** She counts something on her fingers the whole time and never says
  what.

**Dylan Eel.** Her grandson, the family's head, thirties, oilskin, a gaff hook,
never looks at the water directly. Voice: *"A sheep a month. It costs less than
a boat."*
- **Wants.** The arrangement to hold. He does not want Long Sarah dead. He wants
  her fed.
- **Knows.** Where Long Sarah lies. The safe paths through the sedge, marked
  with sticks only the family can read.
- **Offers.** Eels, the family's stilt-house as a place to sleep, fishing and
  swimming training.
- **Talks about.** The paths. The sheep. Long Sarah, and why you should leave
  her be [saw:sarah]. The Eel-Man, who was his father [killed:eelman, he thanks
  you and does not mean it].
- **Tell.** He turns everything you hand him over twice, looking for water
  damage.

**Morwen Eel.** Dylan's daughter, twelve, fearless, the only one who goes out
at night. Voice: *"Grandmother says the river went south. I have been south. It
did not stop there."*
- **Wants.** To know what is under the water. She is Tam's opposite: she does
  not want to leave, she wants to go down.
- **Knows.** That the flood has a floor, and there are houses on it, and one of
  them has a light in it.
- **Offers.** She will show you the sunk houses for a hook of your own.
- **Talks about.** The light under the water. The Hollow Hills' door, which she
  has seen from the fen's south edge [act:2].
- **Tell.** She is up a pole when you arrive and comes down head first.

### Kiln Heath

**Enid Brick.** The brickmaker's widow, fifties, clay under every nail, lives at
Fallow's Adit and sells pots. Voice: *"That one I bricked from outside. You
want to know why, you can come back when you have been down the Cellars."*
- **Wants.** Nobody to open the bricked kiln. Then, when you have, for him to
  be buried.
- **Knows.** Which kiln has the stair. What her husband became. What the
  cultists pray to.
- **Offers.** Pots, clay, the Adit's copper and tin, pottery training to 40.
- **Talks about.** The kilns and which is which. The Cellars' bandits, who buy
  her pots. The bricked kiln [entered:cellars]. Her husband, in full, and asks
  you to end it [act:3]. Whether it is the same thing as the keeper, when she
  hears what a keeper is [act:4].
- **Tell.** She throws a pot on the wheel throughout and it is always the same
  pot.

**Caradog Wynn.** Ex-cultist, twenties, sitting in the sun outside the Cellars
with his robe off, shaking. Voice: *"They say open. All day. Open, open. I said
it too. Then I heard what answered."*
- **Wants.** To go to the Vale and never see a kiln again. He will walk with you
  as far as the hedge and stay there.
- **Knows.** The Cellars' layout, the Kiln-Warden's face, and the word.
- **Offers.** Nothing. He is the game's first turncoat.
- **Talks about.** The Cellars. The word "open," which he says once and will
  not again [sets: heard:open]. Hesk, whose name the Abbot Below spoke, which
  Caradog took for a saint's [act:2, when you mention Hesk].
- **Tell.** He flinches at the kilns every time the wind moves the ash.

**Rhys the Torch.** Bandit captain of the Cellars' upper level, forties, a
burned scalp under a cap, honest in the way thieves are. Voice: *"We were here
first. They came up from below. We keep the stair and they keep to it. You are
welcome to change that."*
- **Wants.** The cultists gone from under him. He will pay you to do it and
  fight you after.
- **Knows.** The Cellars' second level, the cultists' numbers, and that the
  bricks at the bottom have a mark on them.
- **Offers.** Stolen goods, a fence for anything you do not want asked about.
- **Talks about.** The arrangement. The mark, which he thinks is a king's
  [saw:weirmark]. A bounty on the Kiln-Warden.
- **Tell.** He counts your gear and names a price for each piece, aloud.

### The Grey Barrows

**Aldous the Quiet.** Necromancer of the Long Barrow, sixties, night only,
robes that were good once, a voice that has not been raised in thirty years.
Voice: *"They call it the Wake. They say it came in on the tide. They are dead
and they are still afraid of it, which I have found instructive."*
- **Wants.** To know. Not to use, not to free, not to heal. To know.
- **Knows.** What the dead say: the word Wake, the tide, that the Vale has a
  keeper. He will not say more about the keeper "because they will not."
- **Offers.** Necromancy training to 60, bone components, the spellbook's
  first pages.
- **Talks about.** The dead and what they say. The Wake [sets: knows:wake]. The
  spiral and its calendar. Ossian's empty coffin, once you have seen it
  [saw:coffin]. The keeper, one sentence [read:tally]. Hesk, whom he calls "the
  hopeful one" and does not trust [met:hesk].
- **Tell.** A skeleton stands behind him the whole time, holding his lamp, and
  he corrects its posture twice.

**Sir Anselm of the Vault.** Bone Knight, keeper of the Barrow Cellars. He
speaks before he fights and this is the game's first talking monster. Voice:
*"My name is Anselm. I was set to keep this row. One is empty. That is not my
failing. Do not touch the others."*
- **Wants.** The row kept. He will let you leave if you do not touch a coffin.
- **Knows.** That Ossian's coffin was never filled. That the king rode east and
  "did not come back to us, and we did not go to him."
- **Talks about.** His duty, once. Ossian, if you say the name [knows:ossian].
  He will not speak twice.
- **Tell.** He straightens the lid of the coffin nearest him before he draws.

**Gwil Mound.** A grave-robber, thirties, camped at the Barrows' outer ring,
cheerful, missing two fingers. Voice: *"Outer rings are safe and poor. Inner
rings are rich and not. I work the middle. So does he, and he is not paying."*
- **Wants.** The Counting Man dead. It counts him every night and he cannot
  sleep.
- **Knows.** The spiral's rule: newer inward. Which mounds have been opened.
  That the dead stand up at midnight and face south east, and where to stand
  so they do not notice you.
- **Offers.** Grave goods, silver, a map of the spiral for a price.
- **Talks about.** The spiral. The Counting Man and its number [killed:
  counting man, he pays]. The midnight rows [saw:rows]. Aldous, whom he thinks
  is harmless and is wrong.
- **Tell.** He weighs a coin on his palm before he answers anything, as if
  deciding what the answer is worth.

### Thornwood

**Brannoc.** The ranger at the wood's edge, fifties, one name, a longbow taller
than he is, speaks to dogs more than people. Voice: *"Seven dens. One alpha.
You will not find the alpha. He will find you. I am telling you where the dens
are so that you are standing somewhere useful when he does."*
- **Wants.** Greymaw dead. Greymaw killed his dogs. All of them, over years.
- **Knows.** The dens. The cold fire and whose it was. The Long Dark, which he
  has been into once and will not describe.
- **Offers.** Archery and tracking training to 60, arrows, hides, a dog if you
  earn it.
- **Talks about.** The dens and the map. The cold fire and the Walker who made
  it [saw:coldfire]. Greymaw's pin [killed:greymaw]. Tam, whom he watches and
  says nothing about until Tam is out of earshot: "he is going to follow you
  whatever you do. Teach him the bow." The Long Dark [act:2].
- **Tell.** He is never looking at you. He is looking past you, at the trees.

**Greymaw** is a Dire Wolf with a Walker's cloak-pin in his hide and he does not
talk, but Brannoc says Greymaw has come to the edge of the wood three times and
looked at the ranger's hut and gone back, "which wolves do not do."

**Delwyn Ash.** A charcoal burner who lives in a clearing a mile in and has
never been taken, thirties, soot to the eyes, talks to the wolves. Voice: *"They
do not take me. I do not know why. I have stopped asking."*
- **Wants.** To be left alone by everyone, wolves included. He is the zone's
  one mystery: the wolves leave him be because the Wake has touched him and
  they know their own.
- **Knows.** Where the pack sleeps by day. That Greymaw "talks in his sleep."
- **Offers.** Charcoal, and the only fire in the wood you can sleep by.
- **Talks about.** The wolves' habits. What Greymaw says in his sleep, which is
  "open" [heard:open, he goes white]. Sir Bryn of the Long Dark, whom he has
  seen at the wood's far edge at full moon [act:2].
- **Tell.** He never faces the fire. He faces the dark and lets the fire warm
  his back.

## Act II: The Kingdom

### The Broken Strand

**Ysolt of the Strand.** Salvager, forties, a needle like a knife, a chart of
the Drowned Coast twenty years in the making. Voice: *"I have lost two boats
and a brother to that coast. You want to go there, you will need a reason I
believe."*
- **Wants.** Aled back. She will not say so until the fire, late.
- **Knows.** The Drowned Coast's channels. The line of the drowned at the
  Steps and who is tenth in it. That Crook's Delve has a boat in it.
- **Offers.** Salvage, sailing and fishing training to 60, passage across the
  sound [act:3, with a reason].
- **Talks about.** Salvage prices. Crook's Delve and the tide mark
  [entered:delve]. The boat and the key [has:key5]. Aled, once [nightfall at
  camp, sets: knows:aled]. The Drowned Coast, and no. Then yes [act:3, after
  Cinderreach or the Frostcrown]. Aled, after [act:3 ending].
- **Tell.** She sews the whole time and never pricks herself and never looks at
  the needle.

**Hedd Salt.** Ysolt's mate, sixties, a face like a cliff, one word where one
will do. Voice: *"Aye."*
- **Wants.** Ysolt not to sail east. He will say this in four words if pressed.
- **Knows.** Every wreck on the Strand and who was in it.
- **Offers.** Rope, tar, boat repair.
- **Talks about.** Wrecks, in one word each. Ysolt's brother: "Good lad. Went."
  The Stack-Queen and what she says: "Don't."
- **Tell.** He is always coiling a rope, and the coil is always perfect.

**Mother Crab** is a Crab the size of a cart and does not talk, but Hedd says
she has been in the Delve "since my father" and he does not want her killed:
"She keeps the harpies off the boats."

**The Stack-Queen.** A Harpy on the tallest stack who has learned three words:
"come up here." Named encounter. Hedd says a sailor a year does.

### The Ember Flats

**Rook.** Mine-captain of the Ember Cut, forties, burn scars to both elbows,
looks at the ore while she talks to you and at you while she talks about the
ore. Voice: *"They carried it north east to the coast. You can see where. The
sand is glass. Do not take the ore into the Vale."*
- **Wants.** Her miners alive to forty. Most are not. She mines a thing she
  knows is wrong because it pays for the ones who are.
- **Knows.** The glass road. The sledge-shape in the deepest cut. That the
  cultists recruit from her shift and she cannot stop them.
- **Offers.** Emberite, iron, mining training to 70, the Cut's forge.
- **Talks about.** Emberite and what it does to skin. The glass road [sets:
  knows:glassroad]. The sledge [entered:embercut]. The Recruiter, whom she
  wants gone. The Vale, and the warning, every time you leave.
- **Tell.** She breaks ore with a hammer through the whole talk and the ore
  glows where it breaks and she never looks at the glow.

**The Recruiter.** Cultist captain on the Flats' edge, thirties, a beautiful
voice, clean robes in a country where nothing is clean. Real name Selwyn.
Voice: *"Brother Hesk says the same as we do. He only says it more politely.
Ask him."*
- **Wants.** You. The Vale's Walker in the Guest's mark would bring the whole
  Vale after.
- **Knows.** Hesk's name and that Hesk has corresponded with the Abbot Below.
  The Sallow Fall's shift changes.
- **Offers.** The mark. Nothing else. Taking it is a fact the world remembers.
- **Talks about.** The Guest, gently. Hesk [sets: hesk:named]. The Kiln-Warden,
  "a zealot, we do not all burn ourselves." The Vale, and what it is sitting on,
  which he knows and should not [read:tally, he smiles].
- **Tell.** He offers you water first, always, and it is always cold, and
  nothing else on the Flats is.

**Cai Blister.** A miner of the Cut, twenties, the youngest on the shift, dying
of emberite and cheerful about it. Voice: *"Rook says forty. I say she is
optimistic. Buy me a drink at the Kiln when I am dead."*
- **Wants.** Someone to remember his name. He will ask you to say it back.
- **Knows.** The Sledge-Master's chamber and the safe way past it.
- **Offers.** He will sell you his emberite share cheap.
- **Talks about.** The Cut's deep floor. The Sledge-Master, who was his
  brother's foreman. Noon the manticore, and the rock it sleeps on, and the
  hour it wakes.
- **Tell.** He coughs, and looks at what he coughed, and puts it in his pocket.

### The Hollow Hills

**Old Wynn.** Last warden of the Weir Tower, eighties, blind, a memory that has

stone. And one where the bracken grows. The rhyme places it, and I was never
taught the rhyme."*
- **Wants.** Someone to have heard the recitation before he dies. Then, when
  he hears Tam's name, for the boy not to go south.
- **Knows.** The kingdom's records, entire. The six weirs and their places.
  That a Weir still lives, when he hears one speak.
- **Offers.** Nothing to sell. Lore training, if the skill exists; otherwise
  only talk.
- **Talks about.** The tower and its road. The recitation [needs: knows:ossian,
  sets: knows:sixweirs]. The seventh weir and the rhyme. Tam's name [met:tam,
  sets: tam:named]. The Stone Garden's tally, "which I was never permitted to
  read, and which the Warden keeps." Hesk, whom he taught to read [met:hesk].
- **Tell.** He recites with his eyes closed and his hand moving as if turning

**Siwan Underhill.** Head of the Hollow Hills hamlet, fifties, speaks in a

you say down here is heard by something."*
- **Wants.** The Painted Door to stay painted. She knows it opens.
- **Knows.** Which doors are real. The Workings' upper level, where her

- **Offers.** Root vegetables, candles, shelter, the hamlet's cellar as a stash.
- **Talks about.** The doors. The Painted Door and the night it opens

  The river under the hills, which is the one from the Sedge Flats

- **Tell.** She puts a finger to her lips before every sentence, out of habit,
  not at you.

**Bedwyr Hollow.** A young man of the hamlet who wants to go down into the
Workings and reclaim them, twenties, loud in a place where nobody is. Voice:
*"My grandfather's table is still set down there. I want to eat at it."*
- **Wants.** The Workings back. He will follow you in if you let him and die if

- **Knows.** The Workings' second level from his grandfather's stories.
- **Offers.** He will carry. That is all.
- **Talks about.** The Workings. The table. The Mire Troll in the granary hall

  quartermaster, he goes down and eats and comes back different and quiet].
- **Tell.** He speaks at full volume and everyone within earshot winces.

### Witchwood

**Fern.** Ten. Dead three hundred years and does not know it. Voice: *"Has my
father stopped being angry? He was angry when he sent me. Will you tell him I
am being good?"*
- **Wants.** To go home to the hall by the sea. To be told her father is not
  angry.
- **Knows.** The rhyme, as a skipping song. That her father was a king and did
  not like being one.
- **Offers.** Nothing. She will give you a petal and it does not fade.
- **Talks about.** Whether you are a servant sent to fetch her. Her father. The
  rhyme [needs: knows:sixweirs, sets: has:rhyme]. The promise [sets:
  promised:fern]. What you said to her father [after Cinderreach, kept or
  broken, and she knows which].
- **Tell.** She brushes petals off the stone as she talks and there are always
  more.

**Idris Coal.** A charcoal burner at the wood's edge, sixties, will not go in,
has not gone in for forty years since the day he saw the child. Voice: *"There
is a girl in there. Do not tell her anything. I told her the year once and she
cried for a week and the blossom fell like snow."*
- **Wants.** Fern left in peace. He is the closest thing she has to a guardian
  and he has never been within a hundred yards of her.
- **Knows.** How to approach the stone: alone, unarmed, and wait.
- **Offers.** Charcoal, and the last fire before the silence.

## The count

Sixty three named people across twenty two zones, six of whom carry the story,
eleven of whom are monsters promoted to a name and a line, and the rest of whom
stand in one place and sell you something and know one thing you need. Every
zone has at least two living voices, except the Stone Garden and the Teeth,
which have one and none by design, because the story needs a place where you
lose your reader and a place where nothing speaks.

Every topic above is a `{ when, say, sets }` row for `story.js`. The gates are
the facts of `10-STORY.md` section 8, and the audit there holds here: every
fact a line sets is read by another line somewhere, and every fact a line
reads is set somewhere, or the thread dangles and the audit throws.
