# G5: the sounds that did not exist, and where each one fires

`public/audio/sfx` had an axe, a pickaxe, a bow, coins, thunder and wolves. It
had nothing for a spell, a cast, a hit by material, a level-up or a boss. The
rule in this repo is that a cue never borrows a wrong recording, so rather than
point `spell_fire` at `thunder-3.mp3` and call it done, the missing sounds are
synthesised: `tools/synth-sfx.mjs` writes 25 WAVs into
`public/audio/sfx/synth/`, and `audio.js` names them.

## The one line main.js needs

```js
const effects = createEffects(sc, { audio });
```

That is the whole wiring. `audio` is the object `createAudio()` already returns,
and it is already in scope at that point in `main.js`. Nothing else changes:
every call into `effects` keeps the signature it had, `audio` is optional, and
with it left out every sound hook is a no-op and the effects are exactly what
they were before.

Two hooks take a new optional argument, and both are back-compatible:

```js
effects.swing(rig, { weapon })     // or { hands: 2 } / { twoHanded: true }
effects.flinch(rig, struckActor)   // a monster row, an actor, an armour piece, or 'bone'
```

`effects.swing(rig)` still means a one-handed swing and `effects.flinch(rig)`
still means flesh, which is what the player is, so the two existing call sites
in `main.js` are correct as they stand and will simply get better when the
weapon and the target are passed.

## The tool

```
node tools/synth-sfx.mjs          # writes public/audio/sfx/synth/, prints what it measured
node tools/synth-sfx.mjs --list   # the recipes, in words
```

Deterministic: every random number comes from `mulberry32` seeded with
`BASE_SEED` and the sound's own name, so two runs are byte-identical and
regenerating is a git no-op. Every file is 44100 Hz, 16 bit, mono, under 2 s,
peak normalised to -3 dBFS, DC blocked at 20 Hz. Each recipe carries its own
`recipe` line saying what it is made of, so nobody has to work it out by ear.

`src/game/audio.test.mjs` decodes all 25 files off disk and checks the format,
the length, the level to within 0.5 dB, the DC offset, the loop seam on
`cast_loop`, and that the bytes committed are the bytes the tool makes today.

## The cue table

The recordings keep their camelCase names. Every synthesised cue is named for
the file behind it, so `CUES.spell_fire` is `synth/spell_fire.wav` and there is
nothing to look up.

| cue | fires from | where | picked by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cast_start` | `effects.cast` | the caster | always, once |
| `cast_loop` | `effects.cast` | the caster | held on `loop` until `stopCast` or the clip ends |
| `spell_fire` `spell_cold` `spell_energy` `spell_poison` `spell_holy` `spell_dark` `spell_physical` | `effects.bolt` at launch, `effects.burst` at impact | the hand, then the target | `spellCueFor(colour)`: the damage type's colour, or the caster's group colour when the spell deals none |
| `aoe_ring` | `effects.ring` | the ring | always |
| `aoe_column` | `effects.column` | the column | always |
| `impact_flesh` `impact_bone` `impact_metal` | `effects.flinch` | the struck body | `materialOf(source)` |
| `swing_light` `swing_heavy` | `effects.swing` | the swinger | `swingCueFor(opts)`, by hands on the weapon |
| `crit` | nothing yet | | combat.js owns crits; the row is ready |
| `heal` `buff` `debuff` | nothing yet | | the ability runtime owns these moments |
| `skill_up` | `progression.lesson` | unpositioned | a skill crossing a round ten |
| `stat_up` | `progression.statLesson` | unpositioned | a stat crossing a round ten |
| `grandmaster` | both of the above | unpositioned | either one reaching 100 |
| `level_boss_phase` | nothing yet | | G3 owns bosses |
| `land` | `main.js` on a fall over 4 m, `abilities_runtime` on a dash or leap | the feet | already wired; the file behind it is now `synth/leap_land.wav` |

Four rows have no caller yet and say so rather than pretending: `crit`,
`heal`, `buff`, `debuff` and `level_boss_phase` belong to files this agent does
not own. They are one line each the day their owner wants them, which is the
whole point of making the sound before the hook.

## What changed that was not a new file

- **`land` stopped being a stand-in.** It was `place-object.opus` pitched down
  to 0.85 with a note saying so. It is now `synth/leap_land.wav`: a sine falling
  90 to 45 Hz for the weight, fourteen grains of lowpassed noise for the grit,
  one bandpass rattle for the gear. `STAND_INS` is down from three to two.
- **`NO_FILE_FOR.hurt` is answered.** It used to say nothing in the folder was a
  person taking damage. There is no `hurt` row even so, and deliberately: what a
  blow sounds like is a fact about what was hit, so `flinch` picks between three
  impacts and a single row would be the lie it used to be.
- **`progression.js` stopped borrowing `discover`.** Every milestone, of every
  skill and every stat, used to play the chime for finding a site on the map.
  `MILESTONE_CUE` is `skill_up`, `STAT_MILESTONE_CUE` is `stat_up`, and
  `GRANDMASTER_CUE` is `grandmaster` at 100 in either.

## No bow release was made

`bow-shot-1.mp3` through `bow-shot-3.mp3` are real recordings of a real bow and
the `bowShot` cue already walks all three. Synthesising a fourth, worse one
would have made the game sound cheaper. `bow-draw-1..2` and `arrow-hit`,
`arrow-miss` are in the same state and are already wired.

## The sounds, and what each is made of

Short form. The long form is beside the code in `tools/synth-sfx.mjs`.

| file | s | what it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cast_start` | 0.60 | three sines at 1, 1.5 and 2.01 of a fundamental sweeping 220 to 880 Hz, with noise sweeping 1.2 to 5.4 kHz over it |
| `cast_loop` | 1.00 | 110 Hz and three harmonics breathing at 2 and 3 Hz, plus 24 quiet partials; every frequency an integer, so one second is one period |
| `spell_fire` | 0.90 | bandpassed noise running 300 to 1800 to 500 Hz, sparse impulses rung at 2.6 kHz for the crackle, a 70 Hz body |
| `spell_cold` | 0.90 | four sine partials from 1046 to 3136 Hz with shortening decays, tremoloed at 19 Hz, frost above 6 kHz |
| `spell_energy` | 0.60 | a 4 ms highpassed crack, then noise through a fast random gate above 1.2 kHz |
| `spell_poison` | 1.00 | noise lowpassed at 1.3 kHz moved by a smoothed random level, with five chirps falling 420 to 140 Hz |
| `spell_holy` | 1.40 | C4 E4 G4 C5 with two harmonics each, 90 ms attack, the most reverb in the set |
| `spell_dark` | 1.40 | saws at 55 and 82.5 Hz lowpassed at 380, with noise chopped by a 37 Hz gate for the rasp |
| `spell_physical` | 0.50 | a sine falling 160 to 48 Hz in a tenth of a second, with 12 ms of lowpassed noise on the front |
| `heal` | 1.20 | C5 E5 G5 arpeggiated 110 ms apart, sine with two harmonics |
| `buff` | 0.70 | C4 E4 G4 together as triangles, lowpassed at 2.5 kHz |
| `debuff` | 0.85 | G4, E flat 4, C4 falling 100 ms apart, saws lowpassed at 1.4 kHz |
| `impact_flesh` | 0.35 | noise lowpassed at 900 Hz over an 85 Hz sine, nothing sharp in it |
| `impact_metal` | 0.70 | a 6 ms strike, then inharmonic partials at 1, 2.76, 5.40 and 8.93 times 620 Hz |
| `impact_bone` | 0.30 | 4 ms of noise into two very narrow bandpasses at 1.1 and 2.7 kHz, and a quiet 130 Hz knock |
| `swing_light` | 0.30 | bandpassed noise, centre 700 to 2600 to 1000 Hz |
| `swing_heavy` | 0.45 | the same shape at 260 to 950 to 380 Hz, half again as long, with a rumble under it |
| `crit` | 0.80 | the metal impact with a struck bell at 1760 Hz and its fifth ringing 0.45 s |
| `skill_up` | 0.45 | E5 then B5, 120 ms apart |
| `stat_up` | 0.60 | C5 E5 G5, 90 ms apart, the same voice |
| `grandmaster` | 1.80 | C5 E5 G5 as brass, then C6 over a held G5 and C4 |
| `level_boss_phase` | 1.80 | C2 at 65.4 Hz with eight harmonics, held a second, bending up a whole tone at the end |
| `leap_land` | 0.80 | 90 to 45 Hz of weight, fourteen grains of grit, one rattle |
| `aoe_ring` | 0.90 | noise whose centre falls 1800 to 300 Hz, going away rather than coming |
| `aoe_column` | 1.40 | a drone rising 80 to 160 Hz with air climbing 400 to 3000 over it |

## Measured

From `node tools/synth-sfx.mjs` and `node src/game/audio.test.mjs`:

- 25 files, 1.83 MB, every peak -3.00 dBFS, largest DC offset 0.108 % of full
  scale (`spell_physical`), longest file 1.800 s (`grandmaster`).
- Two runs of the tool produce identical SHA-256 for all 25 files.
- `cast_loop` seam step 0.003510 against a mean adjacent step of 0.008098, so
  the join is quieter than the material either side of it.
- Spectral centroids separate the pairs that have to be different:
  `swing_light` 5298 Hz against `swing_heavy` 3807; `impact_bone` 946 Hz against
  `impact_flesh` 745 and `impact_metal` 1498; `spell_physical` 127 Hz against
  `spell_cold` 9231; `level_boss_phase` 231 Hz on a 67 Hz fundamental.
- All 8 spell cues are reachable from a real ability: over the 78 rows of
  `abilities.js`, `spellCueFor(colourFor(id))` gives physical 35, energy 11,
  dark 9, holy 8, buff 6, cold 5, fire 2, poison 2.

Nobody has listened to these yet. Everything above is a measurement, not a
judgement: Fable verifies by ear.
