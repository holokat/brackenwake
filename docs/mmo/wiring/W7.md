# W7 wiring: the wand, the staff, and no spell without one

The user's line, verbatim:

> To be a mage and cast spells, player needs a Staff or Wand, wand is 1 handed,
> staff is 2 handed. spells cannot be cast while a sword is equipped for
> example. so when a new player starts, we have to start them with some sort of
> melee weapon, a ranged weapon - bow maybe with some arrows, and a mage weapon
> so they can choose which to train up with.

Two new weapon bases, one new rule in `weaponCheck`, four kits changed, and a
settler's kit that hands every character all three ways to train in its first
minute.

---

## 1. The two bases

`src/mmo/items.js` WEAPONS, which is now twenty one rows rather than nineteen:

| id | name | skill | hands | damage | speed | weight | STR | type | notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `wand` | Wand | magery | 1 | 2 to 6 | 2.2 s | 1 | 0 | energy | `casts` |
| `staff` | Staff | magery | 2 | 5 to 11 | 2.8 s | 4 | 10 | energy | `casts` |

Both are deliberately the worst thing you can put in that hand. The wand's tap
is the weakest swing in the table after fists; the staff's is under the
quarterstaff it resembles (6 to 12). That is the price of being the only thing
a spell will go through.

**"Staff" and "Quarterstaff" do not collide.** They are different rows with
different ids, different skills and different damage, and the pack shows two
different names. `docs/WEAPONS.md` carries both.

### `casts` means something now

`casts: true` used to sit on the quarterstaff and be read by nothing at all,
which is precisely the decoration CLAUDE.md was written about. It is now the
flag that puts the `focus` tag on a base, and `abilities.js` refuses every
spell without one. The quarterstaff no longer claims to cast; it is a
Macefighting stick, exactly as the brief asked.

`auditItems()` proves the flag and the tag exist only together, that every
focus is a `mainHand` weapon trained on a casting skill, and that there is at
least one of each hand count. `FOCUS_BASES` and `isFocus()` are exported for
the consumers.

### Why a focus carries the `staff` tag as well as `focus`

`kinds: ['equipment', 'weapon', 'melee', 'focus', 'staff', 'onehand']`.

`affixes.js` `candidatesFor` matches an affix's `kinds` list against the base's
tags, and its magic list is `MAGIC = ['robe', 'staff', 'ring', 'amulet']`. That
is the word affixes.js already uses for "a magic implement", and affixes.js is
not this agent's file to edit. A wand tagged only `focus` would have been the
one magic weapon in the game that could not roll Spell Damage, Cast Speed,
Lower Mana Cost, Spell Channeling, Mage Armour, Faster Summons, Longer Buffs or
Cast Recovery.

Measured: `candidatesFor('wand')` and `candidatesFor('staff')` each return 38
candidates including all 8 of the magic group; `candidatesFor('longsword')`
returns 30 with none of them. A rare wand at seed 12345 rolls "Hit Lightning
12" and "Life Leech 5", so rarity and affixes work on it like any other weapon.

**Left for whoever owns affixes.js:** renaming that `MAGIC` entry from `staff`
to `focus` would let the two tags separate. Nothing is broken today.

### The bone staff moved

`bone_staff` was built off the quarterstaff (`{ ...BASES.quarterstaff }`), which
was harmless while `casts` meant nothing. It is not harmless now: the
necromancer's whole kit is that staff, so the opening would have started unable
to cast a single one of the ten necromancy spells it unlocks. It is built off
the mage's staff instead. Same two hands, same 5 to 11, now a focus, still
called Bone Staff and still tagged `bone`.

### Magery is not a combat skill, and that is written down

`COMBAT_SKILLS` is derived from the `Combat*` groups of `skills.js` and Magery
sits in `Magic`, so `auditItems`'s "every weapon trains a combat skill" would
have refused both bases. Rather than loosen that rule, `CASTING_SKILLS =
['magery']` is a named exception and `WEAPON_TRAINS` is the union. A third
exception has to be written down too.

Consequence worth knowing: `combat_rules.weaponSkillId` reads the held weapon's
skill, so hitting something with a wand trains **Magery**. That is intended: a
mage out of mana who starts whacking is still training the right skill.

**The rule is one line: an ability that costs mana is a spell** (`isSpell`,
exported and driven both ways in the test). Not the group: Bandage sits in the
healer group and costs a bandage, Meditate costs nothing, and neither is
something you cast.

### The three judgement calls

**Consecrate Weapon keeps wanting a weapon.** It costs mana, so the mana rule
would make it want a wand, and its entire effect is a `weaponEnchant` on the
blade you are holding. A wand with holy on it enchants nothing. `deriveNeeds`
tests `effectUsesWeapon` **before** the mana test, so this row alone falls
through to `anyMelee`. It is the only row in the table where both are true.

**Resurrect wants a focus, even though it is Healing 80.** It costs 40 mana, so
it is magic by the same rule as everything else. Its `anyOf` also allows a
Chivalry 85 route. The bandage route to raising the dead is not this row at
all: it is the Bandage ability's own `resurrectAt: { healing: 80, anatomy: 80 }`
block, which costs a bandage, is not a spell, and needs nothing in hand.

**The two mana-free passives are not spells.** Arcane Mastery and Elemental Kin
are `cost: { mana: 0 }`. A passive that switched itself off unless a wand were
in the hand would be a surprise, and neither is cast.

Everyone abilities are untouched: Bandage, Meditate, Camp, Jump and Sprint all
still need nothing at all, driven with a wand, with a longsword and with empty
hands.

### The refusal

Modelled on G2's Lunge sentence, and it names what is in the hand:

```
Fireball wants a wand or a staff in your hand, and you are holding a Longsword.
Fireball wants a wand or a staff in your hand, and your hands are empty.
```

### `anyMelee` had to be narrowed

"Any weapon you swing" was "any weapon that is not ranged", and a wand passed
it. So Power Strike, Whirlwind, Leap Slam, Consecrate Weapon and Poison Blade
all quietly accepted a wand. The branch now excludes a focus:

```
Power Strike wants a weapon in your hand, and a Wand is not one you swing.
```

### The eight kinds

`NEEDS_KINDS` is eight now, and every one of the 78 rows has one:

| kind | n |
| --- | --- |
| `melee` | 5 |
| `anyMelee` | 5 |
| `unarmed` | 1 |
| `ranged` | 5 |
| `focus` | 34 |
| `shield` | 2 |
| `instrument` | 6 |
| `none` | 20 |

`auditAbilities` throws if a row that costs mana needs anything but `focus` or
`anyMelee`, if a `focus` row names a base that is not a focus, or if a `focus`
row costs no mana.

**08-POLISH-CONTRACT.md now disagrees with the code.** Its line "a spell needs
nothing in hand but a rooted cast" is the clause the user overruled. That
document is not this agent's to edit; the comment at the head of the rule in
`abilities.js` says so out loud.

## 3. The kits

| opening | before | after | in the hand after creation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mage | quarterstaff, robe set, 4 mana potions | **staff**, robe set, 4 mana potions | `staff` |
| Sorcerer | quarterstaff, robe, 6 reagent pouches | **staff**, robe, 6 reagent pouches | `staff` |
| Necromancer | bone staff, dark robe, skull | unchanged (the bone staff is a focus now) | `bone_staff` |
| Healer | mace, cloth set, 20 bandages | **wand**, mace, cloth set, 20 bandages | `wand`, mace in the pack |
| Blank | dagger, cloth | dagger, **wand**, cloth | `dagger`, wand in the pack |

The healer's wand is listed **before** the mace and that ordering is load
bearing: `creation.js` wears the kit in the order it arrives, so the wand takes
the main hand. A healer's first two abilities (Heal at Chivalry 20, Cleanse at
35) are spells.

**The paladin was left alone.** Chivalry 45 unlocks four spells, but the kit is
a longsword and a buckler and a holy book, and a wand in it would only ride in
the pack anyway. The settler's kit puts one there in the first minute.

`docs/mmo/04-CLASSES-ABILITIES.md`'s kit column was updated to match on those
three rows. The wand is declared in `openings.js` `INVENTED_ITEM_BASES` (now
eleven, was ten) because 03-ITEMS-LOOT.md has no wand row; the staff is
doc-sourced, because 03's slot table already says the main hand takes "a
staff".

### Two bugs found on the way

**`creation.js` PREFERRED had no wand or staff entry**, so the mage's staff
resolved to `null` and was silently never made. The mage and the sorcerer came
out of `planCharacter` with an empty main hand and nothing in the pack, and the
only thing that said so was the shortfall line. Two entries added.

**The necromancer's skull was taking his staff out of his hands.** A recent
change (c7af0e2) made `inventory.equip` send a two hander to the pack when
something is raised in the off hand, which is right for a player who chooses it
and wrong at creation. `creation.test.mjs`'s "everyone has something in hand"
was **already failing on main** because of it. `creation.js` now has the
mirror of its own `twoHandBusy` guard, so the skull stays in the pack and is
named among what stayed behind, exactly as the paladin's holy book is.

## 4. The settler's kit

`src/game/app/systems/inventory.js`, granted once at boot:

| line | count | held back when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `axe` | 1 | already owned |
| `pickaxe` | 1 | already owned |
| `shortbow` | 1 | already owned |
| `arrow` | 40 | already owned |
| `skinning_knife` | 1 | already owned |
| `wand` | 1 | any focus is already carried |
| `dagger` | 1 | any melee weapon is already carried |

"A melee weapon" is drawn on the same line `abilities.js` draws for `anyMelee`:
a held weapon that is neither a bow nor a focus. So an axe counts and a staff
does not, which is why the three pure casters (mage, sorcerer, necromancer) get
the dagger and nobody else does.

### The guard changed, and why

It was `if (!owns('axe') && !owns('pickaxe'))`. That reads as "has not had the
kit" for ten openings and lies about the eleventh: **the artisan starts with an
axe and a pickaxe, so the artisan never got a bow, arrows or a skinning knife
at all** and nobody had counted it. The record is now the skinning knife, which
is the one base in the kit that no opening's own kit carries.

The trade: the exploit surface is one item rather than two. Selling the
skinning knife and rebooting would grant the kit again, where before it took
selling both the axe and the pickaxe. Same class of hole, one step wider, and
worth it for an opening that was getting nothing. A durable flag would be
better, but `state.hydrate` keeps only the document's own keys, and `state.js`
is not this agent's file.

### Measured after boot

| opening | pack |
| --- | --- |
| warrior | `arrow x40, axe, bandage x6, pickaxe, shortbow, skinning_knife, wand` (7) |
| mage | `arrow x40, axe, dagger, pickaxe, potion x4, shortbow, skinning_knife` (7) |
| blank | `arrow x40, axe, pickaxe, shortbow, skinning_knife, wand` (6) |

Booting the same warrior a second and a third time changes the pack by nothing
and says nothing. All eleven openings end able to train melee, bow and magic;
nobody is handed a second focus, and no kit that already had a blade is handed
a spare dagger.

A full pack now says what did not fit, rather than eating the wand in silence,
and does **not** mark the kit as given, so a settler who makes room still gets
it.

## 5. Models and art

`weapon_models.js`:

* **wand**, 0.37 m stated, **0.372 m measured**, 848 triangles. A turned rod
  with three metal bands, a lathed cap and a stone in three claws. It builds
  its own butt cap rather than calling `pommel()`, whose silhouette starts at a
  fixed 20 mm radius and so turns inside out on anything this slender: that is
  what the whole-kit winding test caught, at -1.66e-6 m3.
* **staff**, 2.00 m stated, **1.995 m measured**, 1128 triangles. A shod shaft
  with a leather grip, a collar, and three arms curving out of it to hold a
  stone clear of the wood. Two metres stands it above the tallest character the
  appearance table allows (2.0 m).

Both use the file's generated PBR families (`c.wood`, `c.metal`, `c.wrap`,
`c.stone`), take a rune line at epic and above, and are far under the 6000
triangle budget (worst in the file is still the halberd at 1496).
`auditWeaponModels()` after the change: 35 bases, 34 built, 24048 triangles.

**`gear_visuals.js` was not edited at all.** `dressRig` already reads
`items.twoHanded()`, so the wand took `HOLD.weapon` and the `one` grip and the
staff took `HOLD.twoHander` and the `two` grip with no special case. That is
now measured rather than assumed: `gear_visuals.test.mjs` checks the grip, the
anchor and the actual hold offsets for both, that a shield beside a staff is
not drawn, and that a wand and a buckler give the `oneShield` grip.

`ui_theme.js` gains a `wand` glyph (a short diagonal rod with a four pointed
star off the tip) and returns it before the `staff` check, since a wand carries
the `staff` tag too. `staff` and `bone_staff` use the existing staff glyph.
`icon_art.js` was not touched: neither base has a painting, so both fall
through to the drawn glyph, which is the documented behaviour.

## 6. Left alone, and why

* **`recipes.js`** has no wand or staff recipe, and none was added. The brief
  said to leave recipes alone unless one already existed; none does, so neither
  base is craftable or forgeable in a metal yet. The 140 forged variants are an
  explicit shape list and were not touched.
* **`affixes.js`**, **`inventory.js`**, **`win_bag.js`**, **`loot_drops.js`**,
  **`hud.js`**, **`win_abilities.js`**: not edited, as instructed.
* **`docs/mmo/03-ITEMS-LOOT.md`** still has no wand row, and its quarterstaff
  row still reads "a mage's stick, casts with it", which is no longer true.
  **`docs/mmo/08-POLISH-CONTRACT.md`** still says a spell needs nothing in
  hand. Neither document is this agent's to edit; both need a pass.
* **No `meditation` field was put on the staff.** "Meditation friendly" would
  have to be read by something, and nothing in the game reads the armour
  tiers' `meditation` fraction today either (the only consumer is one affixes.js
  tooltip line). Adding an unread field is the exact failure CLAUDE.md names.
