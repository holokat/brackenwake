# U5: the map has a right hand column, and it is the index to the picture

Owned and changed: `src/game/win_map.js`, `src/game/win_map.test.mjs`, and this
note. Nothing else was edited. `src/world/zones.js`, `src/game/compass.js`,
`src/game/win_character.js`, `src/game/ui_theme.js`, `src/game/hud.js`,
`src/world/sites.js` and `src/game/app/systems/world.js` were read and left
alone.

Nobody else has to call anything. The panel contract is unchanged, `main.js`
registers the same `panel`, and the same `ctx` it always handed over is enough.

---

## 1. What was wrong

The MAP tab drew 640 pixels of continent on the left and left the whole right
half of the codex black. The only words on the page were the footer:

> 16 km across, 1 place found, 1 of 21 regions walked

A picture cannot say which way a thing is, or how far, or what the country
under you is called. Every one of those questions was answerable out of state
the panel already had in its hand and threw away.

## 2. What is there now

Two columns. The picture, its footer and its one gold line of speech on the
left; the index on the right, in the codex's own furniture: `bw-panel` for the
plate, `bw-hdr` for the small caps headers over a gold rule, which is the same
header THE PACK wears on the character page, Cinzel for names and numbers and
Cormorant for anything said in a sentence.

**WHERE YOU STAND.** The region you are in and its authored `line`, its danger
band in words, the ground under your feet in words, the way to the nearest town
you have found, and the way to your mark with a `clear waypoint` button beside
it when there is one. Under the header, in grey, the one sentence that tells a
player the map is a control and not a picture: click it, or any row, to set the
mark.

**REGIONS, n of 21 walked.** All twenty one, nearest first. A region you have
walked into shows its name, its danger band in the game's own words, and the
bearing and distance from where you stand; clicking it puts the mark on its
centre. One you have not shows the word `unwalked`, dimmed, with the bearing and
the distance and **not the name**, which is exactly what the hatching on the map
is withholding, and it is not clickable.

**PLACES FOUND, n.** Every site the character has discovered, nearest first,
each with its kind, its name and the way to it, each a click that sets the mark.
Empty is a sentence, not a gap.

**WHAT THE COLOURS MEAN.** Twelve swatches: the eight biomes the ground is
painted with, the road, the hatching, the mark and you.

## 3. The three rules this had to keep

**One list, not two.** The footer said "1 place found" out of a loop inside
`drawMap`. A second loop in the column would eventually have disagreed with it,
and the player would have been the one who noticed. There is now one exported
`foundPlaces({ field, cx, cz, span, discovered })`; `drawMap` calls it and so
does `sideModel`, and the test drives both and asserts the two counts are one
number.

**One waypoint field, one save.** A row click and a map click both go through
`panel.setWaypoint(hit)`, which is the only code in the file that writes
`character.waypoint` and the only code that calls `state.touch('waypoint')`. The
test clicks the map, then clicks a region row, and checks the second wrote the
same field the first did and touched the same key. `clearWaypoint()` is its
opposite and says which mark it took off.

**Nothing says a number it has not counted, and nothing says a bare number at
all.** A region's danger is `dangerWords(zone.danger)`, built from zones.js's own
`DANGER_WORD`: a one tier band is the word the arrival banner uses, a two tier
band says both ends ("dangerous at best, very dangerous at worst"). The test
asserts no digit appears in either. Bearings and distances are compass.js's:
`bearingOf` for the heading and `distanceText` for the metres, so the strip
across the top of the screen and the list in the codex can never round the same
walk two different ways. The eight point words are keyed off compass.js's own
`POINTS`, and a ninth point fails the audit.

## 4. Every click owes the player words

| what you click | what happens | what is said |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the map, on a found place or walked region | the mark moves | the gold line under the map, and a toast |
| the map, on anything else | nothing | "Nothing there you have been to." |
| a walked region row | the mark moves to its centre | the gold line, and a toast |
| an unwalked region row | nothing, it is not a target | the row already reads `unwalked` |
| a place row | the mark moves to it | the gold line, and a toast |
| `clear waypoint` | the mark comes off and the save is touched | "The mark on X is cleared. The compass has nothing to point at now." |

## 5. `auditMapWords()`, which runs at import

The class fix, not the case fix. Four biomes once shipped with a pickaxe that
did nothing because only one of them was checked. So a ninth biome, a ninth kind
of place or a ninth compass point now throws at import rather than drawing a
colour with no name:

- every biome `field.js` can return has a colour, a word for WHERE YOU STAND and
  a swatch in the key, and every biome swatch in the key is a colour the map
  really draws
- every kind of place `zones.js` knows about has a colour and a word for a row
- every point of the compass has a word
- every monster tier has a word for a region row

The test drives it both ways: it deletes `GROUND_WORD.snow`, proves the audit
names snow, puts it back and proves it is green again, and does the same to
`KIND_WORD.mine`.

## 6. Measured

`node src/game/win_map.test.mjs`, on this machine:

- **153 checks pass, 0 fail.** 62 of them were there before and are untouched.
- the draw is still inside its budget: `128x128 samples at 125 m: 45.6, 42.1,
  43.4 ms, worst 45.6 of a 130 ms budget`. Three consecutive runs gave worsts of
  66.1, 44.7 and 45.6 ms. The budget was not loosened.
- the whole column costs **0.199 ms a call** against the 130 ms the picture
  beside it is allowed, over 50 calls with three places and three regions found.
  It is rebuilt with the picture, once every `REDRAW_S` (2 s) and on every click.
- a character that has walked 3 of 21 regions and found 2 places draws **21
  region rows** (3 clickable, 18 dimmed) and **2 place rows**, and the footer's
  count and the list's length are the same number.
- **not one of the 18 unwalked names appears anywhere in the column's text**,
  and no dimmed row carries a `data-zone`. Walk into all 21 and all 21 names
  appear and no row is dimmed, with the word `unwalked` left only in the key.
- the key's colours are the draw's colours: a real draw is recorded and
  `ROAD_COLOUR`, `WAYPOINT_COLOUR`, `PLAYER_COLOUR`, `HATCH_INK` and
  `HATCH_WASH` are each proved to have been used by it. The player arrow and the
  hatching used to be literals inside `drawMap`; they are constants now, so the
  key cannot drift from the picture.
- `npm test`: ALL SUITES GREEN.

## 7. Two judgement calls worth reviewing

**WHERE YOU STAND names the region even when the walked list does not have it
yet.** `zoneAt` says which zone you are inside; being inside one is the
definition of having walked into it. In the running game there is no gap:
`world_runtime` calls `discovery.checkZone` on a 400 ms throttle and
`app/systems/world.js` pushes the id onto `character.zones` the moment it fires,
so the header and the REGIONS row agree by the next redraw. The REGIONS list
itself is walked **only** when the discovery record says so, so the list and the
hatching on the map can never disagree.

**The nearest town falls back to a hamlet.** If you have found no town but have
found a hamlet, the row names the hamlet and calls it a hamlet. A hamlet is not
a town and is not called one, but it is the nearest thing with a roof, and it is
better than a blank. With neither, the row reads "none found yet".

## 8. Not verified

No browser. The whole column is driven through the real `build`, `render`,
`redraw`, `setWaypoint` and `clearWaypoint` against a small fake document, and
real click events are fired at real rows, so the behaviour is measured. What is
**not** measured is the look: the grid falling to one column under 1080 px, the
two line rows sitting where they should, the swatch shapes (the ring, the
arrow's clip path, the hatch's repeating gradient), and whether 21 region rows
plus the places plus the key make the codex body scroll further than is
comfortable. That needs eyes on a screenshot.
