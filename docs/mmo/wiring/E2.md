Wave B, task E2. Written by the E2 builder. The contract is
`docs/mmo/wiring/WAVE-B.md`, section "E2 events"; the world is
`docs/mmo/14-KALDERA.md`, which names all eight of these under "Out in the open".

## The clock, and why it is not the one in the brief

An in-game day is one day cycle: `DAY_CYCLE_MS`, 25 real minutes. An in-game
hour is 62.5 real seconds. Midnight is where `win_dev.js`'s own slider says it
is, so `hourAt(t)` and the bench's Time of day label read the same number and
setting the bench to 15:00 puts the Bone Wind up. Measured: the worst
disagreement between `dayFractionAt` and `clockOffsetFor` over five slider
positions is 0.

Dusk and dawn are **solved**, not taken from `NIGHT_FRACTION`. The naive answer
is a fifth of the cycle centred on midnight, 21.6 to 2.4, and it is wrong by
over an hour at each end: `dayFactorAt` puts its raw curve through
`raw * 1.4 - 0.2`, so the light is already under the world's night threshold
(0.4, `NIGHT_BELOW` in `app/context.js`) well before the sun is under the
horizon. `solveNight()` bisects the day clock at import and gets 21.82 to 2.18,
4.36 in-game hours of dark, rounded INTO the dark at both ends so a dusk to
dawn window is night at its first millisecond and at its last. The Ghost Tide
was measured over its whole window at five second steps: 0 samples with the
light up.

One bug this class of care caught: hour buckets computed as
`t / DAY_MS - MIDNIGHT_PHASE` floored 6.000000000000001 and 6.999999999999999
to the same hour, and a wandering boss stood at one place for two hours in
three. Everything now counts milliseconds from `EPOCH_MS` and the arithmetic is
exact.

## What is on the calendar

Eleven rows: five named events and one march per realm that holds the Legion.

| id | when | how long | where |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tithewagon` | every 3rd day | hours 7 to 17 | the Kingsroad, end to end, the direction alternating |
| `ghosttide` | the moonless night, one in 8 | dusk to dawn | up the shore from the Coliseum to the Reef Stair |
| `bonewind` | every day | hours 15 to 16, one in-game hour | out of the Rib Cathedral across the Boneyard, a 200 m zone |
| `blossomfall` | every 4th day | hours 5 to 6, at dawn | the whole of the Verdant Deep |
| `longnight` | every 7th day | dusk to dawn twice over, 8.7 hours | the whole of Frostreach |
| `legionmarch:<realm>` | every day, 6 realms | hours 9 to 15 | between that realm's Legion holds |

Plus two that are not windows: the Brass City's circuit (`brassCityAt(t)`, four
wells, a day at each and an hour between, four in-game days to the circuit) and
the wandering bosses (`wanderPointAt(id, t)`, one place an in-game hour).

**The Legion's march is a written table with a guard.** WAVE-B said "camps in
the sheet whose `contains` names the Legion", and the sheet does not call them
all camps: the Legion holds a road in the Greenwold, a ford in the Deep, a pass
in the Stormpeaks and a harbour town at the Throne. So `LEGION_HOLDS` is
written out, and `auditEvents()` refuses any place whose own row in `realms.js`
does not name the Legion, AND refuses to let a realm with two or more such
places be left out. Both directions are driven in the test by corrupting the
table each way. Six realms march; the Saltmarch, the Boneyard and the Sunken
Kingdom name the Legion nowhere and have none.

## What was measured

`node src/mmo/events.test.mjs` (57 passed, 0 failed):

- A full week of the clock at one minute steps, 175 samples: no event ever
  running twice at once, and nothing ever standing outside its own realm.
- Counted over that week: 7 Bone Winds, 7 marches in each of 6 realms, 3 Tithe
  Wagons, 2 Blossom Falls, 1 Long Night, 1 Ghost Tide.
- The Bone Wind's own hour, at two second steps over a day: 62.0 s live of a
  62.5 s hour, which is the step size and not a leak.
- All 11 windows at four instants each: live at the first millisecond, live at
  the last, dead at the instant it ends, dead a minute before, dead a minute
  after. Phase 0 at the first and above 0.999 at the last.
- **The wagon on the road.** The Kingsroad is the longest chain of real road
  inside the Greenwold: 679 m over 3 points, at seed 20260904. 301 samples
  along it through `field.sampleAt(x, z).road`: worst road strength **1.000**,
  0 samples off road. 101 samples at the hours it actually rides: the same.
  Building the chain from the roads' own polylines rather than their two
  endpoints was the difference between 1.000 and 0.000 over the middle 60% of
  it, which is what a bend point is.
- The Brass City over a whole circuit, 1200 samples: all four wells visited,
  23.02 in-game hours standing a day, 0.98 moving, halfway through the walk it
  is 636 m from the well it left and the wells are 1273 m apart.
- The wanderers over two in-game days at eighth-hour steps: Rimemouth 47 moves
  in 48 hours, never outside Frostreach, never at a place whose realm is not
  its own. Noon exists at 8 of 96 quarter hours, all of them between 11 and 13,
  and at none of the other 88. `isNoonHour` agrees with the schedule at all 96.

`node src/game/events_runtime.test.mjs` (60 passed, 0 failed), against a fake

## What is NOT verified

- **Nothing has been seen in the browser.** No dev server was started and no
  save was touched. Every number above is from node.
- **No event has a body of its own yet.** The Tithe Wagon is five soldiers
  walking a road; there is no cart, no horses and no oxen, because the models
  are not E2's files. The Brass City is a position; V1 builds the machine.
- **The sky does not darken.** `scene.js` has no `setDayScale`, so the Bone
  Wind's dust and the Long Night's dark are worked out, readable through
  `events.dayScale()`, said out loud, and invisible. The runtime warns once on
  the console rather than pretending. The hook is below.
- **Foraging does not read `harvestYield`.** The Blossom Fall's buff is real
  and lands on the actor; nothing consumes it yet. `harvestYield` has been an
  unconsumed bonus since W1. The hook is below.
- **The escort cannot be taken away** until `monsters.despawn(key)` exists. The
  runtime warns once and leaves the bodies standing where the event left them.
- The Ghost Tide does not double the ordinary spawn rate, the Long Night does
  not put night rows in the day roll, and something does still spawn in the
  Brass City's path. All three are rolls inside `monsters.js`, which E2 does
  not own; each is listed below. What E2 does instead is real: the Tide puts
  six drowned on the shore itself, the Long Night puts wolves in Frostreach by
  day, and the Brass City reports where it is so the roll can ask.
- The wandering bosses the player has found live in the events runtime and are
  lost on reload. One line of `state.js` fixes it, below.

## Wiring Fable has to do

### 1. `src/game/app/systems/index.js`: register the system

```js
import { events } from './events.js';
```

`FRAME_ORDER` and `SYSTEMS`, with `events` after `world_life` and before
`dragon`:

```js
export const FRAME_ORDER = ['world', 'player', 'emotes', 'combat', 'abilities', 'inventory', 'world_life', 'events', 'dragon', 'ui', 'context_menu', 'dev', 'input'];

export const SYSTEMS = [world, player, emotes, combat, abilities, inventory, world_life, events, dragon, ui, context_menu, dev, input];

export { world, player, emotes, combat, abilities, inventory, world_life, events, dragon, ui, context_menu, dev, input };
```

Its `deps` are `['world', 'player', 'combat', 'ui']` and are declared in the
system itself. It reads `frame.worldNow`, adds `sc.clockOffset`, and writes
`ctx.get('ui').panelCtx.events`, which is where `win_map.js` reads the marks.

Why there: it moves bodies by their `ai.home`, and the monster runtime steps
those bodies inside `combat.update`, which runs earlier in the list. Moving a
home after the step means the column walks on the next frame rather than this
one, which is right. Anything that draws from it runs later still.

### 2. `src/game/scene.js`: one hook, four lines

Inside `createScene`, beside `clockOffset`:

```js
let dayScale = 1;      // what an event does to the daylight: the Bone Wind darkens, the Long Night holds the dark
```

and in the returned object, beside `setClockOffset`:

```js
    /**
     * An event's hold on the daylight. 1 is the ordinary sky; 0 is night at
     * noon. `src/game/events_runtime.js` is the only caller: the Bone Wind's
     * dust and the Long Night's dark.
     */
    setDayScale(v) { dayScale = Number.isFinite(v) ? clamp01(v) : 1; },
    get dayScale() { return dayScale; },
```

and `dayFactor` becomes:

```js
    dayFactor(nowMs) { return dayFactorAt(nowMs + clockOffset) * dayScale; },
```

That one multiplication reaches everything, because `world.dayFactor` is
`sc.dayFactor` and the sky, the lights, the fog, `world_runtime`'s fauna and
the monster runtime's night flag all read it. No second path.

### 3. `src/game/monsters.js`: one line in the returned object

Beside `despawnDev`:

```js
    /** Take one spawned body away by its key: an event's escort when the event ends. */
    despawn(key) { return despawn(key); },
```

`despawn` is already the private function that does it. Without this an event's
escort is left standing in an empty field for the rest of the session.

### 4. `src/game/monsters.js`: two rows in `TAG_RULES`

Both tags are wired now and the table still says they are not:

```js
  noonOnly: ['events.js', 'wanderPointAt returns nothing outside NOON_FROM_H to NOON_TO_H, so the row is nowhere at any other hour'],
  wanders: ['events.js', 'the row route is walked one place an in-game hour; events_runtime spawns it within 600 m and moves ai.home along it'],
```

### 5. `src/game/foraging.js`: make `harvestYield` mean something

`harvestYield` has been in `UNCONSUMED_BONUSES` since W1 and the Blossom Fall
needs it. In `createForaging`, beside `skillValue`:

```js
  /** What the actor's Harvest Yield is worth: 1 ordinarily, 2 in the Blossom Fall. */
  const yieldFactor = () => 1 + num(actorOf()?.bonuses?.harvestYield);
```

and in `harvest`, `const count = yieldFor(skill, plants);` becomes:

```js
    const share = yieldFor(skill, plants);
    const factor = yieldFactor();
    const count = Math.max(1, Math.round(share * factor));
```

and after the `added` line, so the player is told which of the two numbers they
got and why:

```js
    if (factor > 1 && added > share) text += ` The blossom is falling, so it came up ${added} instead of ${share}.`;
```

That wires the affix as well as the event: a Bountiful ring will double a patch
the day it is added to the loot table.

### 6. `src/game/state.js`: remember the bosses you have met

One key on the character, so a wandering boss stays on the map across a
reload. Where the other arrays are hydrated:

```js
  if (!Array.isArray(character.bosses)) character.bosses = [];
```

and then in `src/game/app/systems/events.js`, `createEvents`'s options gain
`found: ctx.character.bosses` and the system's `save` writes
`ctx.character.bosses = ev.found()`. Say the word and E2 writes that half; the
runtime already takes `opts.found` in and hands `found()` back out.

### 7. `src/game/monsters.js`: the three rolls E2 cannot reach

Not needed for anything above to work. Each is a read of
`ctx.get('events')` inside the spawn roll:

- **The Ghost Tide doubles the roll.** In `rescan`'s chunk roll, while
  `events.at('ghosttide', now)` is live and the chunk is in the Sunken Kingdom
  or on the Saltmarch shore, take twice the usual number.
- **The Long Night puts night rows in the day.** `spawnRollFor` decides day or
  night from the day factor; with hook 2 in, `sc.dayFactor` already returns 0
  over Frostreach on that night and the night list is chosen with no further
  change. Worth checking rather than assuming.
- **Nothing spawns in the Brass City's path.** `events.brassCity(now)` gives
  `{ x, z, moving }`; while `moving`, refuse a spawn within 200 m of the line
  between the well it left and the one it is walking to.

### 8. For V1

`events.brassCity(t)` returns `{ x, z, station, stationIndex, next, moving, k,
until }` in world metres. `BRASS_STATIONS` is the four wells. The body should
be at that point and kneeling while `moving` is false, which is 23 hours in 24.

## One thing E2 would like and did not ask for

`monsters.spawnAt` keys everything `dev:<id>:<n>`, so the dev bench's "Clear
spawned" takes an event's escort away with it and an event's escort shows up in
anything that counts bench spawns. It works, and nothing is wrong with it, but
a second argument (`spawnAt(id, x, z, opts)` with an `opts.key` prefix) would
keep the two apart. Not needed for Wave B.
