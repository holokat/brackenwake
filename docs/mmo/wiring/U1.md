# U1 wiring: the look, the codex, the paper doll, the zone banner

Files owned: `src/game/ui_theme.js` (new), `src/game/paperdoll.js` (new),
`src/game/windows.js`, `src/game/hud.js`, `src/game/win_character.js`,
`src/game/win_bag.js`, `src/game/win_skills.js`, `src/game/win_abilities.js`,
their `*.test.mjs`, and this note. Nothing in `src/mmo/` was touched, and no
other agent's file was edited.

## What changed, in one paragraph each

**One look, in one file.** `ui_theme.js` holds the palette (near black stone,
parchment, gold, the dark red tab plate), the two fonts (Cinzel for headers and
small caps, Cormorant Garamond for body and quotes, both from Google Fonts with
serif fallbacks), and every piece of ornament as an SVG data URI **generated in
code**: the nine slice filigree border, the corner marks, the rule with a
diamond in it, the arch behind the paper doll, the parchment sheet, sixteen
line icons and thirty three item glyphs. Nothing is downloaded and no file is
added to `public/`. `injectTheme(doc)` puts the sheet in once and is called by
`hud.js` and `windows.js` on their own, so main.js does not have to.

**One window, six tabs.** The manager grows a codex: Character, Inventory,
Skills, Abilities, Crafting, Map are pages of a single framed window with a tab
strip. Talk, Trade, Settings and Dev keep their own themed windows. The panel
contract is untouched, so W5's five panels and `win_dev.js` were not edited.

**A is never a window key again.** `RESERVED_KEYS` is `w a s d q e space shift
control tab`. `register()` refuses any of them, warns which panel asked, and
registers that panel with no key. The abilities page moved to **P**.

**The doll is the live rig.** The Character page renders the actual player rig
into a canvas inside the arch, so a helm shows the moment it goes on.

**The zone banner.** `hud.zone(name, sub)` throws the place name across the
upper third for three seconds. main.js already calls it.

## What main.js must call

| when | call | state today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the place changes | `hud.zone(text, sub)` | **already wired**, `main.js` in `updatePlace` |
| once a second while dev is on | `hud.setDev(true, { fps, frameMs, draws, tris, monsters })` | **already wired**, `main.js` in `tickFps` |

Nothing else is needed. In particular **the paper doll needs no wiring**:
`win_character.js` builds it itself out of `ctx.sc` and `ctx.player`, both of
which main.js already puts in `ctx`. If either is missing the arch says "no
likeness to draw" rather than showing a black rectangle.

Two optional lines, neither required:

```js
// share one doll between the sheet and the HUD portrait plate
ctx.paperdoll = createPaperdoll(sc, () => player, { width: 244, height: 400 });
hud.setPortrait(ctx.paperdoll.canvas);      // the plate top left takes it

// the console
window.__bw = { ...existing, get codexTab() { return windows.tab; } };
```

## What each page shows

**Character.** Three columns. Left: the name in a large serif, a title line, an
italic line of the character's own, then ATTRIBUTES (five icon rows) and COMBAT
STATS (health, mana, stamina, armour, attack, defence, dodge, parry, critical
chance, critical damage). Centre: the arch with the live rig in it, seven slots
down each side, a motto plate beneath. Right: RESISTANCES, the purse and load,
and a parchment note written out of the numbers just counted. Click a worn item
to take it off; drag between the doll and the pack as before; hover for the full
tooltip in the item's rarity colour. **No level anywhere**, because the game has
none.

**Inventory.** The pack as gilded squares with rarity borders and a drawn glyph
per item, counts on stacks, a filter row (all, weapons, armour, consumables,
materials) that fades what does not match rather than removing it, and a gold
and weight row. **Double click equips or uses.** One click still identifies a
mystery. Right click still opens the menu. Drag still works.

## Titles, quotes and the numbers

The title under the name is the highest skill's word once that skill reaches
**30**, else the opening's name, else "Wanderer". All fifty two skills have a
word and `auditTitles()` throws at import if a fifty third appears without one.

`sheetOf(character, actor)` asks the rules layer for everything: `stats.derived`
for the pools, `inventory.js` for weight, carry, armour and resists, and
`combat_rules.js` itself for attack, defence, dodge, parry and the crits. When
`ctx.actor` exists it IS the fighter those functions are handed, so the sheet
and the fight read one record.

## Invented here, and why

| thing | why | where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Attack and Defence instead of "hit chance"** | the reference sheet prints a hit chance. Hit chance is `0.5 + (attack - defence) * 0.005`, a question about **two** fighters, and a character sheet knows one. Printing 50% or 95% would be a number that means nothing. The two halves the roll is actually made of are printed instead | `win_character.js` `sheetOf().fight` |
| **No silver and no gems in the purse** | the reference has three currencies. This game has gold. A silver counter reading 0 forever is decoration | `win_character.js`, `win_bag.js` |
| **Seven slots each side of the arch, not six** | the reference flanks with six and six for twelve. This game wears fourteen. `auditDoll()` counts the cells against `items.SLOTS` and also refuses a lopsided arch | `win_character.js` `DOLL` |
| Titles per skill, and the 30 threshold | nothing in the documents names a title. 30 is where `skills.js`'s gain curve first slows, which is the first point a skill is a choice rather than a dabble | `win_character.js` `TITLES`, `TITLE_AT` |
| A quote and a motto per opening | the reference has both. Eleven openings, eleven lines each, with a fallback pair for an opening nobody has written for | `win_character.js` `QUOTES`, `MOTTOES` |
| The banner's 0.6 s fade out | the brief gives 0.4 in and 2 held and no out | `hud.js` `BANNER` |
| `RESERVED_KEYS` | the brief says A must never be a window key. One key would be a fix; the list is the class | `windows.js` |
| The doll's 15 fps, 30 degree lens, 0.86 fill | none of the three is in any document. They are in `paperdoll.js` as named constants and `paperdoll.test.mjs` measures what they produce | `paperdoll.js` |
| Item glyph tints | `gear_visuals.js` has the 3D material colours. These are separate 2D presentation values chosen to read on dark stone. `auditTints()` fails if an armour tier appears with no tint | `ui_theme.js` `MATERIAL_TINT` |

## How the doll gets into its own picture

The world's lights are the sky's, so at midnight the doll would be a
silhouette. So: the live rig group is moved into a small lit scene of its own,
rendered to a `WebGLRenderTarget`, and moved back, **all inside one synchronous
call**. No frame is drawn in between, so the world never sees the character
missing. The target is read with `readRenderTargetPixels` and put into a 2D
canvas, rows reversed because WebGL hands back the bottom row first.

Three consequences worth knowing:

- Reading the framebuffer stalls the GPU, which is why the doll draws at
  `PAPERDOLL_FPS` (15) and only while the Character page is open. It does
  nothing at all when the page is closed.
- `shadowMap.enabled` is deliberately **not** toggled around the render.
  Flipping it recompiles every material in the scene.
- **In dev mode the doll is empty**, because `dev.js` calls
  `player.setVisible(false)` and the doll renders the same group. This is
  recorded rather than worked around: the fix belongs in whoever owns the dev
  camera, not here.

## Audits that run at import

Five, all of which throw rather than shipping a lie:

- `ui_theme.auditGlyphs()`: every base in `items.js` has a drawing, and only a
  material may fall through to the plain parcel. 166 bases today.
- `ui_theme.auditTints()`: every armour tier has a tint. Six today.
- `win_bag.auditUsable()`: the two named consumable bases (`potion`,
  `bandage`) exist; everything else that can be eaten is read off
  `items.js`'s own `kind` and `use`, so the cooked meals another agent added
  mid-pass were usable without an edit. 69 bases today.
- `win_character.auditDoll()`: a cell per slot, once each, labelled, and the two
  flanks within one of each other. 14 today.
- `win_character.auditTitles()`: every skill makes somebody, and no title names
  a skill that does not exist. 52 today.

## What was measured

`npm test`, plus these numbers from the suites:

- **windows**: 107 checks. C opens the codex on Character, B turns to
  Inventory, P to Abilities, **A does nothing at all**, K, V and M turn to
  theirs, the same key again closes, Escape closes and then opens Settings, a
  standalone window closes the codex and the codex closes it back, and exactly
  one thing is open at every step. The last section builds the **real** four
  panels in the **real** codex against a fake document and clicks a real tab.
- **paperdoll**: 36 checks. A 1.8 m rig in a 244 x 400 frame stands 3.9056 m
  off, decided by height; the same rig in a frame of aspect 0.2 stands 8.2018 m
  off, decided by width; distance scales exactly with height; the eye sits at
  0.936 m. With no renderer and no document it draws nothing, throws nothing,
  and says why.
- **bag**: 57 checks, including four real `dblclick` events fired at real cells
  through the real `createInventory`: a sword lands in `equipment.mainHand` and
  the pack slot empties and it is said out loud; a potion reaches `ctx.useItem`
  with `{ pack: 1 }`; with no `useItem` hook the pack says so as a refusal; an
  ingot changes nothing and says "it goes into something, it does not go on
  you"; an empty slot does nothing and says nothing.
- **hud**: 105 checks. The banner is `in` at 0.5 opacity at 0.2 s, `held` at
  1.0 at 1 s, `out` at 0.667 at 2.6 s, and `done` at 3.1 s, read off the real
  element's opacity after real `hud.update` calls. The dev badge prints all
  five numbers main.js measures, and `setDev(true)` with no stats still says
  "fly mode", which is how `dev.js` calls it.
- **character**: 60 checks. Attack and defence equal `combat_rules`'
  `attackSkill` and `defenceSkill` to the rounding; a `+12 hit` affix on the
  actor moves attack by 12; a document with no stamina is not treated as
  exhausted, and one with stamina 0 loses exactly 20 attack.

## What is NOT verified

Everything below was written and none of it has been seen on a screen. There is
no browser in this pass.

- **Nothing here has been looked at.** Every claim about the look, the arch, the
  filigree, the fonts and the layout is a claim about CSS and SVG that has been
  parsed by nobody. Fable's screenshots are the first real test.
- **The paper doll has never rendered a pixel.** The framing arithmetic is
  measured; the render target, the scene swap, the pixel readback and the row
  flip have run zero times. If the doll is upside down or blank, that is where
  to look. `doll.trouble` says why it is blank when it knows.
- **The Google Fonts link has never been fetched.** Offline or blocked, the page
  falls back to Palatino and Georgia, which is a different look, not a broken
  one.
- **The codex's height on a short screen.** `min(72vh, 760px)` on the page body
  and a 30 px filigree border were not tried at 900 px tall.
- **Drag and drop between the doll and the pack** was not exercised in the DOM
  tests; only click and double click were. The drag code is unchanged from W3.
- **The tab strip with all six panels registered** was tested with four. The
  crafting and map panels are W5's and were not imported into my suite.
- **`npm test` at the time of writing had four red suites**, all of them pure
  timing budgets in files U1 does not own: `src/world/roads`, `src/world/grass`,
  `src/world/flora` and `src/game/win_map`. They pass on a quiet machine and
  fail while other agents are building. Every suite U1 owns is green.
