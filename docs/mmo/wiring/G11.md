# G11: a weapon's effects reaching the thing it hits

The user's words: "Weapon effects should actually apply to enemies. A freezing
effect on a sword should freeze the enemy according to the effect chance of the
sword or the overall stats on the character."

They did not, and the reason was written down a wave ago. W1.md section 7 lists
thirty bonus keys that `actor.recompute` sums and nothing reads, and twelve of
them are this: the seven hit effects, Parry, Damage Reflect, Thorns, Stun Resist
and Stamina Leech. A "Freezing Longsword of Frost" carried `bonuses.hitFrost`
at 0.30, the tooltip promised it, and no line of code anywhere in the tree ever
read the key. This wave reads all twelve.

| file | what changed |
| --- | --- |
| `src/game/hit_effects.js` | new. The pure half: what fires, how big, in what colour, and the words for it |
| `src/game/combat.js` | `afterBlow`, the parry fold, `applyStatus` growing a slow factor and a stun resist, `float`, `onHit`, `recompute` |
| `src/game/hit_effects.test.mjs` | new, 111 checks |
| `src/game/combat.test.mjs` | untouched. Its 167 checks still pass against the changed file |

Nothing else was touched. `actor.js`, `combat_rules.js`, `affixes.js`,
`monsters.js`, `abilities_runtime.js` and `main.js` are exactly as their owners
left them.

## 1. What main.js has to do

### The one new argument, and the one new listener

```js
import { recompute } from './actor.js';

const combat = createCombat({
  floaters, hud, audio, progression,
  recompute,                 // NEW. actor.js's own. Only Hit Dispel wants it.
  rng: Math.random,
});

// NEW. The particles for a hit effect, at the body it landed on.
combat.onHit(({ defender, colour, kind, damage, fired }) => {
  if (!fired) return;                       // a dispel that found nothing
  effects.burst(defender.pos, colour, damage > 20 ? 1.4 : 1);
});
```

`colour` is a NUMBER (`0x6fd0ff`), which is the form `effects.burst` wants.
`hit_effects.cssColour(colour)` is the same value as a CSS string if anything
else needs one. The callback fires once per effect, on the frame it landed,
never on a miss. Its shape:

```js
{ attacker, defender, effect, kind, colour, damage, fired, now }
```

`kind` is one of `fireball lightning frost harm drain fatigue dispel poison
reflect thorns`. For `reflect` and `thorns` the two actors are swapped, because
the thing being hit is the swinger.

**Without `recompute` nothing breaks.** Hit Dispel then marks the buff expired
instead of removing it, and `abilities.update` drops it on its next tick. See
section 4.

### Nothing else changes

`combat.queueSwing`, `combat.queueSpell`, `combat.update` and the frame order
are all exactly as W2.md and G3.md left them. `monsters.swingAt` still calls
`queueSwing` and gets the hit effects for free, because they run inside
`landSwing`.

### Worth having on `window.__bw`

`combat.rollHitEffects(attacker, defender, { damage: 20 })` rolls the list
without applying it, for poking at a sword in the console. Remember the
module-instance trap: read it through `__bw.combat`, not through a fresh
`import()`.

## 2. What fires, and how big

Everything is keyed off the attacker's SUMMED bonuses, which is `actor.js`'s
`recompute` output, which is gear plus buffs. That is the whole of the user's
"the effect chance of the sword or the overall stats on the character": there is
only one number and it already includes both.

| line | chance | magnitude | where the number came from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hit Fireball | `bonuses.hitFireball` | 10 to 20 fire | the brief |
| Hit Lightning | `bonuses.hitLightning` | 12 to 24 energy | the brief |
| Hit Frost | `bonuses.hitFrost` | slow 30% for 4 s, and a freeze of 1.5 s | the brief |
| Hit Frost | | 8 to 16 cold on top of it | **INVENTED** |
| Hit Harm | `bonuses.hitHarm` | 8 to 16 physical, no armour and no resist | the brief |
| Hit Life Drain | `bonuses.hitLifeDrain` | heals 30% of the blow, rounded | the brief |
| Hit Fatigue | `bonuses.hitFatigue` | 10 to 20 stamina | the brief |
| Hit Dispel | `bonuses.hitDispel` | one buff, the most recent | the brief |
| Vampiric | always | heals 25% of the blow | 03: "all damage leeches 25%" |
| Everfrost | always | slow 40% for 4 s, freeze chance 0.15 for 1.5 s | 03 for the 40%, the rest **INVENTED** |
| Poison Blade | always, while the enchant lives | poison at the enchant's level | W4's `weaponEnchant` |

### Scaling, which is 03's own clause

03-ITEMS-LOOT prices the seven as "a 10 to 40% chance per hit of a small spell
at half the wielder's skill", and gives no other number at all. The wielder's
skill for a spell off a blade is Magery, and the per point rate is
`combat_rules.SPELL_PER_INT`, **imported rather than re-guessed** so a hit
fireball and a cast fireball grow at the same rate:

```
scale = 1 + (Magery * 0.5) * SPELL_PER_INT        100 Magery -> x1.40
```

Measured: a 10 to 20 fireball is 10 at the bottom of the roll with no Magery and
14 with 100.

### The freeze ramp

The brief pins one end, "at 100 Magery freezes solid", and says nothing about
the other. **INVENTED** as a straight line: `freezeChance = Magery / 100`. So a
warrior's Freezing sword slows and never freezes, a mage's freezes every time,
and a 50 Magery character freezes half the time. Measured all three ways.

Everfrost does NOT use that ramp. **INVENTED**, 15% flat, on the argument that a
legendary sword should freeze in the hands of a warrior who has never cast
anything, which is the difference between a power and an affix.

### Resists

A typed hit effect is reduced by the defender's own typed resist through
`combat_rules.resistOf`, which is the same reader `combat_rules.damage` uses.
Measured: 50% fire resist halves a hit fireball, 20 to 10.

Two deliberate exceptions, both said out loud:

* **Harm ignores the physical resist too.** "Ignores armour" would have been
  free, because none of these go through the AR term at all, so a line whose
  whole promise is that nothing stops it would have been stopped by a
  breastplate's 12%. Measured: 16 of 16 against a 50% physical resist.
* **Resisting Spells does not apply.** `resolveSpell` reduces a cast spell by
  it; a charge off a blade is not a cast. **INVENTED**, and it is the one place
  a hit effect and a real spell differ in arithmetic.

### Where the rng is drawn

One draw per line the attacker actually carries, in the fixed order of
`HIT_EFFECT_IDS`, then one or two more for a line that fired. **A line the
weapon does not carry draws nothing**, so a plain sword spends no rng at all and
a seeded sequence is the sequence it looks like. Measured: a sword with no hit
lines makes 0 draws and returns 0 effects.

## 3. What a landed blow now costs both sides

All of it is in one function, `combat.afterBlow`, and all of it runs only on a
blow that took health. A miss, a dodge and a parry arrive with `res.damage` at
zero and leave with nothing said.

| bonus | what it does now | who reads it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `parry` | folded into `bonuses.defence` on a copy of the defender, see section 5 | `landSwing` |
| `damageReflect` | a fraction of the blow back through `hurt`, on a swing or a spell | `afterBlow` |
| `thorns` | a flat number back through `hurt`, melee only | `afterBlow` |
| `stunResist` | shortens a stun, and only a stun | `applyStatus` |
| `staminaLeech` | percent points of the blow, onto the attacker's stamina | `afterBlow` |

**Reflect and Thorns go through `hurt`**, which owns the death, so a swinger can
genuinely be killed by the thing it just hit. Measured: a full reflect off a big
blow kills the swinger and `onDeath` names him.

**Thorns is melee only.** Spikes on a breastplate need something to prick, and
an archer thirty metres off is not touching it. Damage Reflect is magic and
comes back down the same line the blow went up, so it reaches the archer.
**INVENTED**, and it is the one asymmetry between the two. Measured both ways in
one fight: the archer takes the reflect and not the thorns.

**Stun Resist is capped at 0.9.** 03 sells the affix at 10 to 50% and says
nothing about stacking, and four pieces of Steadfast armour would add to 200%.
**INVENTED**: a stun always lands for at least a tenth of its time. Measured: 4 s
becomes 2 s at 50%, and 0.4 s at 400%.

**Stun Resist does not shorten a freeze.** A freeze is a `root`, not a `stun`,
and Steadfast is sold as Stun Resist. Measured: 1.5 s stays 1.5 s.

## 4. Three paths that had to be traced, and what was found at the end of them

### The slow factor

`monsters.speedOf` reads `status.slow.factor` and falls back to its own
`SLOW_DEFAULT` of 0.30 without one. `combat.applyStatus` built its entries out
of `level`, `perSecond`, `until` and `nextTick` and **carried no factor at all**,
so a 40% Everfrost slow would have arrived as a 30% one and nobody would have
noticed. `applyStatus` now carries `spec.factor` onto the entry, and keeps the
stronger of two when a second slow lands. Measured: a 60% slow really moves a
rat at 40% of its run, and a 30% slow landing next to a 40% one leaves the 40%
standing.

### The enchantment counted in hits

`abilities_runtime.doWeaponEnchant` writes `hitsLeft: 5` for Poison Blade, and
`abilities.update` only ever expires an enchantment **by its clock**. Nothing in
the tree decremented `hitsLeft`, so a hits-based enchantment lasted for ever. A
landed blow is the only thing that can spend one and this file is where a blow
lands, so `combat.spendEnchant` does it, says "The last of it goes off the
blade" when the fifth goes, and does **not** spend one on a spell. Measured:
gone after exactly five landed blows, and a cast spends none of them.

### The dispelled buff

This is the one that could have shipped as a bug. `abilities_runtime.expire` is
the only thing in the tree that calls `recompute` when a buff drops, so a buff
spliced out of `actor.buffs` here **without** a recompute would take its +STR
into the grave and leave the bonus applied for ever. So `stripOneBuff` has two
paths:

* with `combat.recompute`, the buff is spliced and the actor recomputed on the
  spot. Measured: one of two buffs gone, `recompute` called once, on the right
  actor.
* without it, the buff is only MARKED expired, `until = -Infinity`, which is
  expired under a clock in seconds and one in milliseconds alike, and its owner
  drops it on the next `abilities.update`. Measured: the array still holds it
  and the mark is on it.

**Wire the `recompute` argument.** The second path is correct but a frame
slower, and only works for an actor something is running `expire` on, which
today is the player alone.

### A note on clocks, found on the way

`abilities_runtime.js` works in SECONDS and `combat.js` works in MILLISECONDS,
and both write `actor.status`. G11 does not compare `actor.enchant.until`
against any clock for exactly that reason: a live enchant is taken as live and
`abilities.update` owns its expiry. **A `control` effect out of W4 writing
`status.stun.until` in seconds would read as long expired to `monsters.speedOf`,
which compares it against a millisecond `now`.** That is not this wave's file
and it is not fixed here, but it is real and it is named.

## 5. Parry, and the one thing that could not be done properly

03 sells a "Parry 2 to 10%" affix on armour, shields and rings, and `actor.js`
sums it into `bonuses.parry` as a fraction. `combat_rules.parryChance` reads
exactly two things, the defender's Parrying skill and its SHIELD's
`parryFactor`, and takes no additive term. That file is finished and not
editable here.

So the bonus is folded into `bonuses.defence` on a shallow copy of the defender,
for the length of one swing, the same trick `swinger()` already uses for an
ability's `hitBonus`. The conversion, written down:

```
hitChance moves by HIT_PER_SKILL (0.005) per point of defence.
The even fight the resolver is built around is HIT_BASE (0.50).
Taking a tenth of the blows off an even fight is 0.05 of hit chance,
which is 10 points of defence.
So one whole point of the parry fraction is HIT_BASE / HIT_PER_SKILL = 100.
```

`PARRY_DEFENCE_POINTS = 100`. A 10% Parry affix is +10 defence. Measured: it
takes exactly 5 points off the hit chance, and a roll of 0.60 that lands on a
bare defender misses one wearing it.

**The cost, plainly: the player sees "miss" and not "parry"**, because the roll
it moved is the hit roll and not the parry roll. When `combat_rules` grows an
additive parry term, `PARRY_DEFENCE_POINTS` and `guard()` both go and the word
comes back.

### And the other one: hit effects on a spell

The brief asked for `landSpell` to run hit effects "only for enchant kinds that
say so". `rollSpellEffects` therefore ignores the affixes and the powers
entirely and asks only `actor.enchant`, and only one carrying `onSpell: true`.

**No ability in `abilities.js` sets `onSpell` today, so this fires for nothing.**
It is one flag away from working and it is named here rather than left to be
discovered: an ability whose `weaponEnchant` effect should ride a spell adds
`onSpell: true` beside its `damageType`, and `abilities_runtime.doWeaponEnchant`
copies it onto `actor.enchant` the way it copies `vs` and `mult`. Measured both
ways: an enchant without the flag fires nothing through a spell, one with it
fires its poison.

## 8. Not done, and named so nobody assumes otherwise

* **No browser.** Nothing in this wave was run in the game. `main.js` does not
  yet pass `recompute` and does not yet call `onHit`, so no player has seen a
  burst. Fable verifies. Section 1 is the whole of the ask.
* **Stormcaller.** "Every hit chains lightning to two others" needs a list of
  other bodies, which a pure function of one attacker and one defender does not
  have. It belongs next to `monsters.coneTargets`, which already knows how to
  find neighbours. `rollHitEffects` returns nothing for it rather than
  pretending.
* **Sunder.** "Ignores 50% AR" is armour piercing and belongs in the damage
  pipeline, as `bonuses.armourPiercing`, not as a proc. Nothing reads
  `actor.powers` for it yet.
* **Phoenix, Windrunner, Archmage, Shepherd, Kingsguard, Undying** are all still
  ids in `actor.powers` that nothing reads. They are not hit effects, so they
  were out of this brief, but `actor.powers` remains a list where eight of ten
  entries do nothing.
* **`actor.marks`.** W4 writes `[{ damageTakenMult, until, from }]` and nothing
  reads it. It is a damage multiplier and belongs where `monsters.swingAt`
  already folds `vulnerability` into `opts.multiplier`. Still unread.
* **`enchant.vs` and `enchant.mult`.** Consecrate Weapon's "half again against
  undead" is carried on `actor.enchant` and read by nothing. G3 wired the
  monster's own `vulnerability` to `holy`, which covers the skeleton case by a
  different road, but the enchant's own 1.5 is still decoration.
* **`onSpell`.** Section 5. The hook exists, the flag does not.
* **The mace's tabled `stun 8%` and the warhammer's `stun 15%`** are in
  03-ITEMS-LOOT's weapon table and nothing rolls them. `applyStatus` now knows
  how to shorten a stun; nothing yet applies one from a weapon.
* **`bonuses.parry` shows as a miss, not a parry.** Section 5.
