THE LIVING VALLEY — ART TO GENERATE
===================================

48 images: 42 card scenes + 6 character portraits.
Drop them in public/ui/story/ using the exact filenames below.
Until a file exists the card shows a labelled placeholder naming it,
so you can work through these in any order.

CARD ART — 4:3, 640x480 is plenty (they render at 340px wide).
Flat, warm, low saturation, matching the game palette. The cards that
land hardest have the least in them: a crate at a gate, smoke on a
ridge, one person at a fence. No text in the image.
