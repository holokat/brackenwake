NOSTRUX HOMESTEAD — ASSET LIST (the ones that make the cut)
==========================================================

FROM 290 INFRASTRUCTURE ITEMS DOWN TO 52 FUNCTIONAL + DECOR.

Why so deep a cut: 290 items shared only ~11 distinct effects. "craft speed"
appeared on ~37 different buildings, "sell bonus" on ~40. And 62 items were
strictly dominated — a cheaper building did the same job better, so buying them
was simply a mistake the game never flagged.

Rule from here: ONE effect lives in ONE category, with a clean ladder and no
duplicate rungs. Everything cut is a decor candidate — see the end.

Descriptions are loose on purpose. Silhouette and materials only; let the image
generator handle the detail.


================================================================
STORAGE — 7  (was 14)
Raises how much you can hold. Wood and stone are NOT in the produce pool at
all — they have their own caps, so an afternoon of chopping can never push a
harvest out of the barn. The Lumber Yard and Stone Yard are the only things
that raise those two.

The cost per stored unit RISES as the rungs climb (1.0 -> 1.8 coins). That is
deliberate: ground space is the real constraint, so you pay a premium for
density rather than covering the farm in sheds.
================================================================

    Small timber shed, double braced doors, stone footing, shingle roof.

Lumber Yard             +250 WOOD only
    Open roofed rack, three shelf levels of stacked boards and logs,
    sawhorses, loading deck.

Stone Yard              +250 STONE only

    low retaining wall.

WORKSHOPS — 10  (was ~37 craft-speed items across six categories)
Kept at ten, but no longer ten copies of the same number.

The five FOOD workshops each speed ONE RECIPE FAMILY, and between them they
cover all eight processors. That is what makes owning several a real
decision: a player who mostly makes cheese wants a different workshop than
one who mostly brews. Previously all thirty-seven did the same thing with a
bigger number, which is why they blurred together.

The other four are not food buildings, and doing food-crafting maths in them
never made sense. They own the MATERIALS side instead — which was the actual
hole in the economy once wood and stone became real resources. Nothing else
in the game improved gathering, and nothing specialised an animal.
================================================================

Tool Shed               x1.15 to ALL crafting — the starter
    Lean-to shelter with tools hung on the back wall, a workbench.

Millstone               x1.5 — Grain Mill recipes
    Round millstone in a timber frame, sacks of grain, dusting of flour.

NOTES
================================================================

Dropped whole as functional categories, because every item duplicated an
effect that now lives elsewhere:

  Protection (15)  fences, walls, storm shelters — all prestige or tiny
                   yield. Better as decor.
  Workers (12)     cabins and halls that auto-watered or auto-collected —
                   duplicates of Water and Machines. Better as decor.
  Science (13)     growth and yield bonuses already covered by Soil and
                   Fields. Best one kept as Agricultural Research Station.
  Aquaculture (12) all small sell bonuses; Commerce covers that.
  Forestry (12)    storage and craft speed; Lumber Yard and Workshops cover it.
  Processing (22)  the craft-speed half is folded into Workshops (each now tied
                   to a recipe family); the sell-bonus half into Commerce.

Repriced to remove dominated rungs — e.g. Milking Shed used to cost 380 for
x2.0 while Shearing Shed gave x2.2 for 320, so nobody should ever have bought
the Milking Shed.
