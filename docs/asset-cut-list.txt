NOSTRUX HOMESTEAD — ASSET LIST (the ones that make the cut)
==========================================================

FROM 290 INFRASTRUCTURE ITEMS DOWN TO 52 FUNCTIONAL + DECOR.

Why so deep a cut: 290 items shared only ~11 distinct effects. "craft speed"
appeared on ~37 different buildings, "sell bonus" on ~40. And 62 items were
strictly dominated — a cheaper building did the same job better, so buying them
was simply a mistake the game never flagged.

Rule from here: ONE effect lives in ONE category, with a clean ladder and no
duplicate rungs. Everything cut is a decor candidate — see the end.

Descriptions are loose on purpose. Silhouette and materials only; let the image
generator handle the detail.

what makes ten of them worth having: with 104 recipes in the game, a player
who mostly makes cheese wants a different workshop than one who mostly
brews. Previously all thirty-seven did the same thing with a bigger number,
which is why they blurred together.

NOTES
================================================================

Dropped whole as functional categories, because every item duplicated an
effect that now lives elsewhere:

  Protection (15)  fences, walls, storm shelters — all prestige or tiny
                   yield. Better as decor.
  Workers (12)     cabins and halls that auto-watered or auto-collected —
                   duplicates of Water and Machines. Better as decor.
  Science (13)     growth and yield bonuses already covered by Soil and
                   Fields. Best one kept as Agricultural Research Station.
  Aquaculture (12) all small sell bonuses; Commerce covers that.
  Forestry (12)    storage and craft speed; Lumber Yard and Workshops cover it.
  Processing (22)  the craft-speed half is folded into Workshops (each now tied
                   to a recipe family); the sell-bonus half into Commerce.

Repriced to remove dominated rungs — e.g. Milking Shed used to cost 380 for
x2.0 while Shearing Shed gave x2.2 for 320, so nobody should ever have bought
the Milking Shed.
