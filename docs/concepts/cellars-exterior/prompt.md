# Concept prompt

Tool: built-in image generation. The generated concept is the reference for the
Blender reconstruction. The screenshot supplied by the user established the
character/tree style; it was described in the prompt rather than sent as an edit.

Create one high-quality game asset concept sheet for Brackenwake: THE OLD CELLARS exterior dungeon entrance. Low-poly 3D geometry, flat shaded large facets and deliberately chunky simple silhouettes, to match a game with stylized small adventurers with oversized angular heads, simple tapered limbs, faceted olive-green tree canopies and reddish brown polygonal tree trunks. This is an art-direction reference to be faithfully built as actual Blender geometry, not a painting texture. Main view fills 75% of image: front three-quarter elevated gameplay view of a ruined cellar embedded naturally in a low, irregular moss-green earthen mound. Broad squat stone arch made of eleven individually shaped ochre-gray wedge stones, with an oversized worn keystone; thick blocky jambs; doorway tall enough for a character (approximately 2.8m clear, 2.8m wide), truly hollow and dark with visible inward/downward stone stair treads. Three worn steps at the threshold, short chunky broken masonry wings tapering into the bank, exposed roots over one shoulder, simple warm amber iron lanterns on stout wooden brackets either side of the door. Mound roof is angular triangles of olive grass and exposed dark earth, with a broken remnant of an old cellar's stone foundation on its rear, asymmetrical silhouette. A crooked pole on left carries a small tattered burgundy Legion banner with simple golden chevron emblem (no lettering). Loose large stones and very restrained grass tufts flank a CLEAR unobstructed approach. NO long isolated flat wall, NO giant tower, NO cathedral, NO glowing magical portal, NO closed door, NO photorealism, NO dense textures, NO tiny clutter, NO UI. Warm muted stone, earth brown, olive green, rusty iron, one amber accent. About 10m wide, 7m deep, 4.5m high overall. Stonework reads at game camera distance. Simple forest-green neutral ground and soft daylight, ambient occlusion. Right 25% of sheet: two smaller orthographic construction views, front and side, exactly the same design and proportions, including hollow entrance and mound silhouette. No text, no measurements, no ornamental frame. The main view must be clear, appealing, buildable and large.
