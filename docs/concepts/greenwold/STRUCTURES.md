Sizes are the base library FOOTPRINT, width by depth by height. A placed piece multiplies these dimensions by its saved scale; the authored settlement buildings currently use 1.25. The words and prompts are MODELS.md's; the spec (glb, metres, Y up, budgets by height, validator) is at the top of that file.

A structure that is not made yet stands in the game as a stand-in body of its footprint, so the layout can be walked before the model exists. Each section is one zone, in the order the first hour meets them; a structure is listed under the first zone that needs it and counted again where it recurs.
